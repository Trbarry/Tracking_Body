Trigger: Planned governed OP Mainnet + Unichain shared-dispute-game migration.
Attacker-controlled inputs: No permissionless migration inputs; after migration anyone may submit a new proof against a new shared game.
Required state: Any pre-migration proof pointing at an old per-chain game.

Candidate compositions: Proof survival across migration; delete before/after migration; stale array; atomic cutover interleaving.

Confidence: High.
Reason it may be benign: The invalidation is explicit documented design. Re-proof overwrites the same submitter mapping cell. No old proof can satisfy registration or retirement checks after the atomic swap.
