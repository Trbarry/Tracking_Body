# PRIM-2401 — Permissionless external-proof finalization can commit a failed direct call

## Mechanism

`finalizeWithdrawalTransaction` selects the caller's proof, but `finalizeWithdrawalTransactionExternalProof` is public and accepts any proof submitter (`OptimismPortal2.sol:494-498,574-607`). After validation the Portal sets the hash-global `finalizedWithdrawals` bit before the target call (`:609-627`). If that call returns false, only withdrawals whose encoded L2 sender is `L2CrossDomainMessenger` revert the whole finalization (`:639-653`). Every other withdrawal remains finalized; nonzero value is returned to the ETHLockbox (`:655-665`).

Consequently, for a direct withdrawal to a contract whose actual execution requirement is above the encoded `gasLimit`, an arbitrary account can choose a low outer gas limit that satisfies `SafeCall`'s floor but causes the target to fail. The legitimate finalizer then has no Portal replay path.

## Preconditions and control

- Production-reachable direct `L2ToL1MessagePasser` withdrawal, not an L2 messenger message.
- A mature valid proof belonging to any submitter.
- Target needs more gas than the withdrawal's encoded minimum (possibly because target state changed).
- Attacker needs no proof of their own and no privileged role; the external-proof entry point supplies the existing submitter.

## Local-fork evidence

Against deployed Portal v6.1.0, a 1 ETH withdrawal with encoded floor 30,000 and a target requiring 260,000 was outer-success/inner-failure at a 350,000 gas cap. `finalizedWithdrawals=true`, target received zero, Portal balance was zero, and Lockbox balance was unchanged. The same shape with adequate outer gas delivered 1 ETH. See `mirror/lane-24/tests/WithdrawalFinalization24.t.sol:275-320` and `FORK_TEST_RESULTS.md`.

## Confidence / benign explanation

High confidence in the primitive, low confidence as a bounty issue. The code explicitly states that all non-messenger withdrawals finalize and that replayability belongs in higher-level contracts (`OptimismPortal2.sol:655-657`). The pinned v9 tests intentionally expect a reverting direct target to emit `WithdrawalFinalized(..., false)` and stay finalized (`test/L1/OptimismPortal2.t.sol:1648-1719`). The withdrawal originator also chose an inadequate floor/direct path. Rejected as REJ-2401, score 3.
