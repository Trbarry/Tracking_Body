PRIM-ID: PRIM-0303 — Portal-held ETH is outside lockbox accounting and only the PAO can move it

Source contracts: `src/L1/OptimismPortal2.sol`.

Exact functions: `donateETH` (`:381-383`), `migrateLiquidity` (`:502-513`), `finalizeWithdrawalTransactionExternalProof` (`:613-615`, `:627`, `:661-665`).

Trigger: anyone calls `donateETH{value}`, or ETH is forced into the portal.

Attacker-controlled inputs: amount; timing (also from inside a withdrawal call).

Required state: none.

State mutation: `address(portal).balance` grows; no `ETHLocked`, no deposit event, no liability.

External calls: none on the way in. Out: `ethLockbox.lockETH{value: address(this).balance}` from `migrateLiquidity`.

Cross-contract dependency: v9 changed the `migrateLiquidity` gate from `_isUsingInterop()` to `_isUsingLockbox()` and added a CGT revert (diff at `OptimismPortal2.sol:502-507`), so the PAO can now sweep on every non-CGT chain.

Broken or weakened assumption: none. Finalization unlocks exactly `_tx.value` and forwards exactly `_tx.value`; it never reads the portal balance.

Candidate compositions: none found that spends it. A later 638 ETH withdrawal left a 20 ETH portal balance untouched.

Evidence: `test_A_receive_isDeposit_zeroValueDoesNotTouchLockbox_donateStaysInPortal`, `test_D_direct_reentrantTarget_matrix_and_strandedDonation` (attacker `migrateLiquidity` reverts; `vm.prank(PAO)` sweep moves exactly 20 ETH), `testFuzz_F_sequenceConservation` (portal balance == Σ donations over random sequences). On-chain portal balance is 0 on all four chains before and after U21.

Confidence: high.

Reason it may be benign: only the donor loses; the ETH is recoverable by the PAO into the lockbox as surplus.
