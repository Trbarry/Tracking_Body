PRIM-ID: PRIM-0402 - forced "finalized-and-undelivered" for NON-messenger withdrawals: exact window

Source contracts: OptimismPortal2 v6.1.0, SafeCall (v9), ETHLockbox v2.0.0
Exact functions: `finalizeWithdrawalTransactionExternalProof` (OptimismPortal2.sol:577-666),
  `SafeCall.callWithMinGas` (SafeCall.sol:121-172), `ETHLockbox.lockETH` (:144)
Trigger: any third party finalizes someone else's mature withdrawal (any submitter's proof) at a moment / with a
  gas amount for which the target call returns false. For `_tx.sender != 0x42…07` the portal then emits
  `WithdrawalFinalized(hash, false)`, keeps `finalizedWithdrawals[hash] = true` and re-locks `_tx.value`
  (OptimismPortal2.sol:657-665). There is no replay path; the ETH becomes unbacked surplus in the lockbox.
Attacker-controlled inputs: timing, tx gas, `_proofSubmitter`.
Required state (one of):
  1. target rejects at that moment (state-dependent or always reverting target) - any gas;
  2. gas starvation: target must still fail after receiving >= G* gas, where G* is forced by the portal's own
     tail (it keeps only 1/64 and needs ~13.2k gas with value, ~3.7k without). Measured, cold (`--isolate`),
     today's rules, deployed code: G* = 830,645 gas (value>0, target burns everything), 231,294 (value 0),
     430,114 (target is a 1-level wrapper that bubbles an inner OOG), 301,794 (2-level wrapper).
     `_tx.gasLimit` is only a floor: the target always gets max(gasLimit, G*) or the outer tx reverts.
State mutation: finalizedWithdrawals true; lockbox balance unchanged; target unchanged.
External calls: lockbox.unlockETH, target, lockbox.lockETH.
Cross-contract dependency: 63/64 rule + portal tail cost; lockbox re-lock.
Broken or weakened assumption: none new - "Messages sent directly to the OptimismPortal have no form of
  replayability" (contract NatSpec). The quantified part is new: for every target that needs < ~0.3M gas
  (wrapped) / ~0.83M gas (direct), a griefer CANNOT force failure by gas, whatever gasLimit the user chose.
Candidate compositions: Amsterdam (EIP-8037) raises the cost of state-creating targets (fresh slot 97,920;
  value to new account 183,600 per the v9 comments) - still far below G*, so in-flight direct withdrawals with
  today's gasLimit are not made forcibly-failable by the fork (argued from the constants, NOT locally
  reproducible). Real exposure = targets that are state-dependent (pausable receivers, allow-listed vaults).
Evidence: `Quant.t.sol::test_forced_failure_thresholds`, `::test_state_dependent_target_thirdparty_timing`,
  `Fab.t.sol::test_gas_burner_*`, `::test_returnbomb`; real material: `Real.t.sol::test_feevault_thirdparty_external`
  (27.24 ETH SequencerFeeVault withdrawal, gasLimit 400k: no failure window, outcome goes NotEnoughGas -> delivered)
  and `::test_feevault_etched_reverting_target_thirdparty` (recipient code SUBSTITUTED with vm.etch -> third
  party burns the withdrawal, 27.24 ETH stays in the lockbox). Output: evidence/lane04-forge-isolate-output.txt.
Confidence: high.
Reason it may be benign: documented design since Bedrock; requires a target that fails for reasons the withdrawer
  chose (user self-harm) - not a protocol bug.
