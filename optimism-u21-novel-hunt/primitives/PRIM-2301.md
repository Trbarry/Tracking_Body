PRIM-2301: Exact-value deposit lock and split mint/call-value encoding

Source contracts: OptimismPortal2, ETHLockbox

Exact functions: `receive`, `depositTransaction`, `lockETH`

Trigger: Any L1 deposit, including plain ETH receive.

Attacker-controlled inputs: `_to`, `_value`, `_gasLimit`, creation flag, data, and `msg.value`.

Required state: ETH_LOCKBOX enabled, nonzero configured lockbox, portal authorized.

State mutation: Lockbox balance rises by exactly `msg.value`; deposit event encodes both `msg.value` (L2 mint) and `_value` (derived transaction call value).

External calls: Portal calls current lockbox `lockETH{value: msg.value}` before emitting the deposit.

Cross-contract dependency: Rollup derivation must interpret the first opaque-data word as mint and the second as call value.

Broken or weakened assumption: `_value` need not equal `msg.value`; treating `_value` as newly created liability would falsely report insolvency.

Candidate compositions: Derivation/parser disagreement; reentrant deposit during withdrawal finalization; CGT zero-value deposits.

Evidence: v9 `src/L1/OptimismPortal2.sol:372-378,730-795`; `src/L1/ETHLockbox.sol:142-151`; fork tests `test_depositMovesValueDirectlyIntoLockbox` and `test_receiveAlsoMovesValueDirectlyIntoLockbox`.

Confidence: High.

Reason it may be benign: The split is explicit in NatSpec and the locked amount equals the minted amount. No mismatch appeared on the deployed fork.
