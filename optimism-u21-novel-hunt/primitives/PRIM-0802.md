PRIM-ID: PRIM-0802 — Classes of messenger withdrawals that are provable but never finalizable after U21
Source contracts: OptimismPortal2 6.1.0, L1CrossDomainMessenger 2.13.0.
Exact functions: `finalizeWithdrawalTransactionExternalProof` (OptimismPortal2.sol:651-653), `relayMessage`
  (CrossDomainMessenger.sol:231, 250-252).
Trigger: finalize a withdrawal with `_tx.sender == 0x42…07` whose inner `relayMessage` reverts whatever the gas.
Attacker-controlled inputs: none on someone else's message (sender, target, nonce, value are fixed on L2 by the L2CDM).
Required state: (a) inner `_target` is the L1CDM or the portal; (b) version-0 migrated message whose legacy hash is
  already in `successfulMessages` (slot 203); (c) pre-existing, version independent: `gasLimit` or payload too large
  for one L1 transaction (e.g. 600 kB message: `gasLimit` 24,031,400).
State mutation: none — the whole finalize reverts; `finalizedWithdrawals` stays false, ETH stays in the ETHLockbox.
External calls: portal -> lockbox.unlockETH -> L1CDM (reverts) -> everything rolled back.
Cross-contract dependency: portal trusts `_tx.sender == L2CDM` to mean "target is the L1CDM" (true: live L2CDM
  rejects relays to itself and to the L2ToL1MessagePasser, test_l2_cdm_cannot_be_made_to_withdraw_to_arbitrary_target).
Broken or weakened assumption: none; v8 finalized these with `success=false` (funds equally stuck).
Candidate compositions: (i) accounting that counts "proven but unfinalized" messenger value as claimable; (ii) with
  the planned shared lockbox, the ETH of class (a) stays in the shared lockbox forever instead of being unlocked and
  re-locked — no balance difference.
Evidence: test_S1_unsafeTarget_neverFinalizes (reverts at 600k/2M/16M/50M gas on l1; finalizes success=false on
  l1pre); test_legacy_v0_inflight (b); measure_tables.md row 17; test_l2_large_message_cost (c).
Confidence: high.
Reason it may be benign: documented by design in the U21 notice and LANE_CONTEXT §6.
