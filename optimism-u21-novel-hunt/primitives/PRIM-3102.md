# PRIM-3102 — Lockbox pause blocks outflows but deliberately leaves bridge inflows open

**PRIM-ID:** PRIM-3102  
**Source contracts:** OptimismPortal2 v9; ETHLockbox v9; SystemConfig; L1CrossDomainMessenger; SuperchainConfig.  
**Exact functions:** `proveWithdrawalTransaction`, `finalizeWithdrawalTransactionExternalProof`, `depositTransaction`, `donateETH`, portal `receive`, `ETHLockbox.lockETH/unlockETH`, messenger `sendMessage`.  
**Trigger:** Guardian pauses the OP chain with identifier equal to the ETHLockbox.  
**Attacker-controlled inputs:** Permissionless callers can continue sending ETH through deposit/receive/donate/messenger send paths during a pause.  
**Required state:** Legitimate local pause. The test uses the real guardian only as setup to create a production-reachable emergency state; no privileged behavior is part of an attack.  
**State mutation:** Proof, finalization, and lockbox unlock revert. Deposits and messenger sends increase lockbox custody; `donateETH` increases portal balance.  
**External calls:** Portal -> lockbox on deposits; L1CDM -> portal deposit.  
**Cross-contract dependency:** All pause views resolve the lockbox identifier, while `lockETH` intentionally has no pause gate.  
**Broken or weakened assumption:** A blanket invariant “all value-moving entry points revert while paused” is false. The accurate invariant is “paused custody cannot flow out; inflows remain accepted.”  
**Candidate compositions:** UI/monitoring treats `paused` as disabling deposits; prolonged pause plus unannounced inflow acceptance; future inflow path unexpectedly coupled to an outflow.  
**Evidence:** `OptimismPortal2.sol:398-400,583-585,730-795`; `/home/user/ethereum-optimism/op-v9.0.0/packages/contracts-bedrock/src/L1/ETHLockbox.sol:142-177`, especially line 158 (“Unlocks are blocked when paused, locks are not”). `test/PortalStateInvariants.t.sol::test_pauseBlocksProofsFinalizationAndUnlocks_butKeepsInflowsOpen` adds exactly 4 ETH custody: 3 ETH to lockbox and 1 ETH donation to portal.  
**Confidence:** High.  
**Reason it may be benign:** Source comments make the asymmetry intentional; no withdrawal or custody-drain bypass was found, and temporary inconvenience is suppressed.
