PRIM-ID: PRIM-3003 — Portal body and Lockbox call execute before ResourceMetering
Source contracts: `ResourceMetering.sol`, `OptimismPortal2.sol`, `ETHLockbox.sol`
Exact functions: `ResourceMetering.metered/_metered`; `OptimismPortal2.depositTransaction`; `ETHLockbox.lockETH`
Trigger: A payable deposit when ETH_LOCKBOX is enabled.
Attacker-controlled inputs: Deposit target, value, gas limit, creation flag and calldata.
Required state: Portal authorized in Lockbox; feature enabled (true both N−1 and N).
State mutation: Lockbox receives ETH/emits before Portal validation and before meter accounting, but all occur in one EVM transaction.
External calls: Portal → Lockbox `lockETH` at `OptimismPortal2.sol:756-759`.
Cross-contract dependency: Lockbox must remain a no-callback authorized sink and revert atomically with later Portal/meter failures.
Broken or weakened assumption: Post-body metering assumes earlier external effects cannot persist or reenter into a useful inconsistent state.
Candidate compositions: A future callback-capable Lockbox, authorization error, or state-dependent reentry before `prevBoughtGas` updates.
Evidence: `ResourceMetering.sol:64-75,128-150`; `OptimismPortal2.sol:741-795`; `ETHLockbox.sol:142-151`; three passing `Lane30ResourceMeteringTest` cases.
Confidence: High primitive, no current bug.
Reason it may be benign: Exact order existed in v8, feature was already active, only authorized Portal can call, Lockbox has no callback, and reverts roll back both contracts.

OLD ASSUMPTION: Effects in the modifier body are safely reversible before metering.
NEW U21 CONDITION: Portal surrounds the old meter with Lockbox custody, although OP already used it pre-U21.
COMPOSITION POSSIBILITY: New external behavior at Lockbox could expose pre-meter state.
REQUIRED ATTACKER CONTROL: Deposit inputs today; exploit additionally needs callback/authorization behavior the deployed Lockbox lacks.
POSSIBLE IMPACT: Meter bypass, deposit DoS, or custody/accounting divergence.
