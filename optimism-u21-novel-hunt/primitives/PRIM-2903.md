# PRIM-2903 — Portal and retired-lockbox authorization is sticky and owner equality is a snapshot

**PRIM-ID:** PRIM-2903

**Source contracts:** v9 `ETHLockbox.sol`, `OPContractsManagerMigrator.sol`,
`ProxyAdminOwnedBase.sol`.

**Exact functions:** `ETHLockbox.authorizePortal`, `authorizeLockbox`, `_authorizePortal`;
`OPContractsManagerMigrator._migratePortal`.

**Trigger:** The common ProxyAdmin owner performs the documented interop migration. The new shared
lockbox authorizes every portal and each retired per-chain lockbox.

**Attacker-controlled inputs:** None for initial authorization. In a composition, a later admin of
one distinct portal ProxyAdmin controls that portal's implementation if ProxyAdmin ownership has
diverged.

**Required state:** At authorization time, target proxy and lockbox resolve to the same
ProxyAdmin owner; portals also resolve through their SystemConfigs to the same SuperchainConfig.

**State mutation:** `authorizedPortals[portal] = true` and
`authorizedLockboxes[oldLockbox] = true`. There is no revocation function. Old lockboxes also keep
their old authorized-portal mappings after being emptied.

**External calls:** Owner/ProxyAdmin introspection; portal SystemConfig/SuperchainConfig reads;
during migration, portal and old-lockbox upgrade/liquidity calls.

**Cross-contract dependency:** The shared lockbox is administered by chain 0's ProxyAdmin, while
OP Mainnet and Unichain portals remain administered by two other/distinct ProxyAdmins that merely
have the same owner today.

**Broken or weakened assumption:** Shared governance equality is checked only when authorization
is added. It is not dynamically rechecked on `lockETH`/`unlockETH`, and authorization cannot be
removed if owners later diverge.

**Candidate compositions:** A future routine ownership separation would give the new owner of one
authorized portal a route to upgrade that portal and debit the entire shared pool; a retired
lockbox remains an authorized source of liquidity to the shared box.

**Evidence:**

- `src/L1/ETHLockbox.sol:122-130`, `180-195`, `218-235`: owner checks, same-owner/SC checks,
  monotonic mappings.
- `src/universal/ProxyAdminOwnedBase.sol:40-105`: how ProxyAdmin and owner equality are resolved.
- `src/L1/opcm/OPContractsManagerMigrator.sol:287-327`: common-owner/common-SC validation.
- `src/L1/opcm/OPContractsManagerMigrator.sol:502-543`: shared authorization, sticky old-lockbox
  authorization, sweep, portal repoint.
- Live state: four distinct ProxyAdmins all owned by
  `0x5a0Aae59D09fccBdDb6C6CcEB07B7279367C3d2A`; OP/Unichain migration eligibility confirmed in
  `mirror/lane-29/LIVE_STATE.md`.

**Confidence:** High.

**Reason it may be benign:** Owner divergence or a malicious portal upgrade is privileged action,
not a permissionless exploit, and the planned interop set is intentionally a shared security
domain. This is not a bounty candidate under the lane rules, but it is a meaningful operational
invariant for future governance changes.
