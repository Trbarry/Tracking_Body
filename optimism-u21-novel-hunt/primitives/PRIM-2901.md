# PRIM-2901 — Authorized portal has amount-unbounded shared-pool debit authority

**PRIM-ID:** PRIM-2901

**Source contracts:** v9 `ETHLockbox.sol`, `OptimismPortal2.sol`.

**Exact functions:** `ETHLockbox.unlockETH(uint256)`,
`OptimismPortal2.finalizeWithdrawalTransactionExternalProof`.

**Trigger:** While the lockbox is unpaused, any address present in `authorizedPortals` calls
`unlockETH(_value)` while that portal's `l2Sender()` equals `DEFAULT_L2_SENDER`.

**Attacker-controlled inputs:** At the primitive level, `_value` is chosen by the authorized
portal and is bounded only by the lockbox's total balance. A normal portal supplies the proven
withdrawal's `_tx.value`; an ordinary EOA cannot make the direct call.

**Required state:** Caller is authorized; shared lockbox has sufficient balance; shared pause is
off; caller portal is idle rather than executing a withdrawal target.

**State mutation:** `_value` ETH moves from the lockbox to the caller portal through
`donateETH`; there is no per-portal contribution or liability counter.

**External calls:** `superchainConfig.isLocalOrGlobalPaused(address(this))`, caller
`l2Sender()`, then caller `donateETH{value:_value}()`.

**Cross-contract dependency:** Correctness depends entirely on every authorized portal exposing
the expected `l2Sender` state machine and calling `unlockETH` only for a valid, chain-bound
withdrawal. The real portal marks the withdrawal finalized and calls `unlockETH(_tx.value)` before
setting `l2Sender` to the withdrawal sender.

**Broken or weakened assumption:** Once multiple portals share one lockbox, authorization is a
full fungible-pool debit capability, not authority over a portal-specific balance.

**Candidate compositions:** (1) a public portal path that makes an idle portal call `unlockETH`
with arbitrary amount; (2) loss of proof chain binding; (3) later divergence of a portal's
ProxyAdmin owner after sticky authorization.

**Evidence:**

- `src/L1/ETHLockbox.sol:153-177` at pinned v9: pause, authorization, total-balance, and
  `l2Sender` checks followed by `donateETH`.
- `src/L1/OptimismPortal2.sol:591-630`: reentrancy state and unlock ordering.
- `src/L1/OptimismPortal2.sol:437-449`: super-game output selection is bound to
  `systemConfig.l2ChainId()`.
- `src/universal/ProxyAdminOwnedBase.sol:79-105`: shared-owner and owner-only checks.
- `mirror/lane-29/LockboxLane29.t.sol`:
  `test_realLockboxRejectsEOAButAuthorizedPortalCanChooseAmountWhenIdle` and
  `test_realLockboxRejectsAuthorizedPortalDuringWithdrawalContext`, run against block N.
- `mirror/lane-29/LIVE_STATE.md`: live authorization matrix and common-owner state.

**Confidence:** High.

**Reason it may be benign:** This is an intentional shared-liquidity trust model. The deployed
portal has no public amount-arbitrary unlock path: direct withdrawal targeting is blocked by the
current-lockbox bad-target check, and the lockbox independently rejects calls while the portal is
executing a withdrawal target. Proofs are chain-bound. No permissionless drain was found.
