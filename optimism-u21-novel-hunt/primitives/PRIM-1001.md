PRIM-ID: PRIM-1001 — Cross-version relay gas budget: L1CrossDomainMessenger 2.13.0 (U21) relays with a 150k reserve while the live L2CrossDomainMessenger 2.2.1 still sizes withdrawals for a 45k reserve

OLD ASSUMPTION: `baseGas()` on the sending messenger and the `hasMinGas`/reserve constants on the relaying
messenger are the same numbers (one shared set of `RELAY_*` constants in `CrossDomainMessenger`), so the
gas the portal guarantees to `relayMessage` (the withdrawal's `gasLimit`) always covers the relay.

NEW U21 CONDITION: v9 splits the constants into LOCAL_*/REMOTE_* (`src/libraries/GasConstants.sol`).
The L1 side was upgraded (L1CDM impl `0x7d4Ec7fB…`, v2.13.0: LOCAL reserved 135,000 + check buffer 15,000,
`GasConstants.sol:60,75`), the L2 side was not (U21 has no L2 hardfork): OP Mainnet L2CDM is still 2.2.1 and
`baseGas(0x,0) = 310,160` (reserve 40,000 + buffer 5,000). v9's own L2CDM 2.4.0 would give 415,160.
Every L2->L1 messenger withdrawal in flight at U21, and every one initiated until the L2 predeploy is upgraded,
is 105,000 gas short of the budget the v9 design assumes.

COMPOSITION POSSIBILITY: (a) Amsterdam / EIP-8037 on L1: v9 itself states an empty value-to-new-account relay
needs 391,338 gas against the new 415,160 budget (`GasConstants.sol:39-42`); the old 310,160 budget is below
that, so such in-flight messages cannot complete at the portal-guaranteed gas. (b) any code path that turns a
`relayMessage` revert into a finalized withdrawal (pre-U21 portals, un-upgraded chains). (c) third-party relayers
that submit exactly the portal minimum.

REQUIRED ATTACKER CONTROL: only the gas limit of a permissionless `finalizeWithdrawalTransaction` call.

POSSIBLE IMPACT: on the U21 contracts, none beyond a retry (see "benign"). On a chain that has the old
messenger/portal when Amsterdam activates: finalized-without-record messenger withdrawal (ETH lost) — NOT a
U21-contract issue and NOT locally reproducible.

Source contracts: `src/universal/CrossDomainMessenger.sol`, `src/L1/L1CrossDomainMessenger.sol`,
`src/L2/L2CrossDomainMessenger.sol`, `src/libraries/GasConstants.sol`, `src/libraries/SafeCall.sol`,
`src/L1/OptimismPortal2.sol` (v9 `ba6fcadb`).
Exact functions: `CrossDomainMessenger.relayMessage` (hasMinGas at :266, CALL at :285), `baseGas`/`_baseGas`,
`OptimismPortal2.finalizeWithdrawalTransactionExternalProof` (:627 call, :651-653 `OptimismPortal_L1CDMCallFailed`).
Trigger: anyone finalizes a proven messenger withdrawal whose `gasLimit` was computed by L2CDM 2.2.1.
Attacker-controlled inputs: tx gas of the finalize call.
Required state: a proven, mature withdrawal with `sender == L2CDM`.
State mutation: `finalizedWithdrawals`, `successfulMessages` / `failedMessages`.
External calls: portal -> lockbox.unlockETH -> L1CDM.relayMessage -> target.
Cross-contract dependency: L2 predeploy version vs L1 implementation version; L1 hardfork gas schedule.
Broken or weakened assumption: "sender-side baseGas == receiver-side reserve". It now only holds after an L2
upgrade that U21 does not ship.
Candidate compositions: Amsterdam activation before the L2CDM upgrade + 7-day proof window; un-upgraded
chains sharing the old messenger; relayer bots with fixed gas multipliers (griefing only).

Confidence: high for today's rules (reproduced); medium for the Amsterdam statement (argued from v9's own
constants and comments, not simulated).
Reason it may be benign: under today's L1 rules the pre-check overhead is far below the remaining 120k slack, so
the relay neither fails nor reverts at the guaranteed gas. Under Amsterdam the worst case is a `relayMessage`
revert, which the U21 portal converts into `OptimismPortal_L1CDMCallFailed` (withdrawal stays unfinalized and is
retried with more gas); a too-low check instead records `failedMessages` (replayable). No path finalizes
without a record.
