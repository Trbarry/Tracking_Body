PRIM-2307: Pause is asymmetric—locks continue while unlocks and finalization stop

Source contracts: OptimismPortal2, ETHLockbox, SystemConfig

Exact functions: `depositTransaction`, `proveWithdrawalTransaction`, `finalizeWithdrawalTransactionExternalProof`, `lockETH`, `unlockETH`, `paused`

Trigger: Global or lockbox-local pause.

Attacker-controlled inputs: Deposits remain permissionless.

Required state: SuperchainConfig reports the lockbox or global pause active.

State mutation: New deposits can still increase lockbox backing and create matching L2 deposits; proof/finalization/unlock revert.

External calls: Pause resolution goes through lockbox -> SuperchainConfig.

Cross-contract dependency: Portal, SystemConfig, messenger, and bridge must resolve the same lockbox pause identifier.

Broken or weakened assumption: Pause does not freeze all state/value ingress; it is an exit brake.

Candidate compositions: Pause-identifier migration; deposit growth during long pause; replay after unpause.

Evidence: v9 `src/L1/OptimismPortal2.sol:398-409,583-615,741-759,824-829`; `src/L1/ETHLockbox.sol:153-170`; `src/L1/SystemConfig.sol:570-576`; fork `test_pauseBlocksFinalizationButNotAdditionalLocking`.

Confidence: High.

Reason it may be benign: Each permitted deposit adds equal backing and liability; paused withdrawals remain backed rather than duplicated.
