PRIM-2303: Authorized portals share an aggregate lockbox with no per-portal quota

Source contracts: ETHLockbox, OptimismPortal2

Exact functions: `authorizePortal`, `_authorizePortal`, `lockETH`, `unlockETH`

Trigger: Any authorized portal locks or unlocks value.

Attacker-controlled inputs: Indirectly, a valid withdrawal value through an authorized portal.

Required state: Portal and lockbox share ProxyAdmin owner and SuperchainConfig; portal is authorized.

State mutation: A single pool balance changes; there is no mapping from portal to contributed or withdrawable amount.

External calls: Authorization reads portal SystemConfig; unlock calls portal `l2Sender` then `donateETH`.

Cross-contract dependency: Solvency depends on every authorized portal's proof domain admitting only its own authentic outstanding liabilities.

Broken or weakened assumption: Isolation is by authorization and proof correctness, not accounting compartments; one corrupted portal could consume the shared pool.

Candidate compositions: Cross-chain proof-domain collision; duplicate L2 chain ID; stale authorization after migration; compromised authorized portal implementation.

Evidence: v9 `src/L1/ETHLockbox.sol:122-178,218-235`; no per-portal value mapping in storage; migrator duplicate-ID rejection at `src/L1/opcm/OPContractsManagerMigrator.sol:291-328`.

Confidence: High.

Reason it may be benign: This is the intended pooled-liquidity trust model. Current OP uses a per-chain lockbox and INTEROP=false; migration rejects duplicate chain IDs.
