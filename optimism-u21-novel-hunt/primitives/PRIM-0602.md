PRIM-ID: PRIM-0602 — Legacy DelayedWETH proxies (<= v1.3.0) ignore the chain-specific pause
Source contracts: un-upgraded DelayedWETH proxies still holding ETH — OP Mainnet 0xE497B094d6DbB3D5E4CaAc9a14696D7572588d14 (v1.0.0, 0.16 ETH), 0x82511d494B5C942BE57498a70Fdd7184Ee33B975 (v1.1.0, 0.08 ETH), 0x323dFC63c9B83CB83f40325AaB74b245937cbdF0 (v1.3.0, 0.08 ETH); Unichain 0xc9edb4E340f4E9683B4557bD9db8f9d932177C86 (v1.3.0, 0.08 ETH)
Exact functions: DelayedWETH.withdraw(address,uint256) of those old implementations: `require(!config.paused())` where config = SuperchainConfig and `paused()` is the legacy no-arg getter = global pause only (SuperchainConfig.sol:135-137)
Trigger: Guardian pauses the chain's lockbox identifier (not the global identifier)
Attacker-controlled inputs: none beyond being a credit holder of a pre-U16 game
Required state: unclaimed credit in a pre-U16 game
State mutation: bond withdrawal proceeds during a chain-specific pause
External calls: SuperchainConfig.paused()
Cross-contract dependency: these proxies were never migrated to the per-chain identifier (neither in U16 nor U21); the OPCM only upgrades SystemConfig.delayedWETH().
Broken or weakened assumption: "DelayedWETH withdrawals are blocked while the chain is paused" does not hold for these four proxies under an identifier pause; it does under the global pause.
Candidate compositions: none of value — total 0.40 ETH of years-old unclaimed bonds; the games are long resolved and use retired ASRs (0x18DAc71c…, 0x1c68ECfb…).
Evidence: test/poc/lane06/PauseMatrix.t.sol::test_legacy_weth_ignores_identifier_pause (PASS): after `SC.pause(lockbox)` withdraw reverts with "insufficient unlocked withdrawal" (i.e. passed the pause check); after `SC.pause(address(0))` it reverts with "contract is paused". Enumeration: test/poc/lane06/Enum.t.sol output in lanes/LANE-06-report.md §5.
Confidence: high
Reason it may be benign: pre-existing since U16, not introduced by U21; dust; no path from these proxies to the lockbox or to current games.
