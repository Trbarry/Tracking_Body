PRIM-ID: PRIM-0301 — Third-party finalization with attacker-chosen gas and call context

Source contracts: `src/L1/OptimismPortal2.sol` (v6.1.0, impl `0xb20b23af…9645`), `src/libraries/SafeCall.sol`.

Exact functions: `finalizeWithdrawalTransactionExternalProof(_tx, _proofSubmitter)` (`OptimismPortal2.sol:577-666`), `SafeCall.callWithMinGas` (`SafeCall.sol:121-172`).

Trigger: any address calls it for any withdrawal that some submitter has proven and that passes `checkWithdrawal`.

Attacker-controlled inputs: which withdrawal, which proof submitter, tx gas, the call stack it is invoked from (EOA, contract, inside another protocol's callback).

Required state: withdrawal proven, mature, game valid, not paused. 16 such real withdrawals existed on OP Mainnet at block 26433900 (list: `evidence/lane03/classified.json`), the largest 638.18 ETH.

State mutation, by case:

| Sender of the withdrawal | Target call | Result |
|---|---|---|
| any direct L2 sender | succeeds | `finalizedWithdrawals = true`, lockbox −value |
| any direct L2 sender | fails, tx has enough gas left | `finalizedWithdrawals = true`, unlock then exact relock; the L2 burn becomes permanent lockbox surplus |
| any direct L2 sender | fails, tx runs out of gas after the call | whole tx reverts, nothing changes |
| `L2CrossDomainMessenger` | L1CDM returns | finalized; value delivered or parked in L1CDM (`failedMessages`) |
| `L2CrossDomainMessenger` | L1CDM reverts | `OptimismPortal_L1CDMCallFailed`, nothing changes (new in v9) |

External calls: `ethLockbox.paused/unlockETH/lockETH`, `systemConfig.isFeatureEnabled`, `anchorStateRegistry.isGameClaimValid`, the target.

Cross-contract dependency: the gas floor is `gasLimit*64/63 + 40_000 (+183_600 when value > 0 and the target has no code, balance or nonce)`; measured total tx-gas floor on the fork is about `gasLimit*64/63 + 200k` (518,078 for gasLimit 313,808; 884,472 for 668,851).

Broken or weakened assumption: none. The withdrawer does not choose when, by whom, or with how much gas above the floor the target is called.

Candidate compositions: a target whose success depends on tx context (caller, `tx.origin`, block timing, being outside another contract's callback) can be driven into the "fails → finalized + relock" row by a third party. That needs a target-side weakness; the portal guarantees only the gas floor.

Evidence: `test_D_direct_targetReverts_valueRelocked_liabilityGone`, `test_D_direct_targetBurnsAllGas_relockStillFits` (gas sweep: revert at ≤520k, finalized-failed with exact relock at ≥560k, never a partial state), `test_C_L1CDMCallFailed_keepsWithdrawalReplayable_mockedRevert`, `test_G_*`.

Confidence: high (real implementations, real proven withdrawals; the direct-withdrawal target was replaced with `vm.etch`).

Reason it may be benign: documented design ("withdrawals are immediately finalized; replayability can be achieved through contracts built on top"). The failure outcome needs the target itself to fail at or above the gas it asked for.
