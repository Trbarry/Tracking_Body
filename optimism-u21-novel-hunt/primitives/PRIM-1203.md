PRIM-ID: PRIM-1203 — omitted sibling checks on privileged paths after the lockbox re-wiring (catalogue for composition; none is permissionless)

Source contracts: src/L1/ETHLockbox.sol, src/L1/OptimismPortal2.sol, src/L1/SystemConfig.sol, src/dispute/AnchorStateRegistry.sol,
  src/dispute/DelayedWETH.sol, src/L1/opcm/OPContractsManagerV2.sol, src/L1/opcm/OPContractsManagerMigrator.sol (v9 @ ba6fcadb).

Exact functions and the check each one lacks relative to its sibling:
  1. ETHLockbox.authorizeLockbox (L184-196) and migrateLiquidity (L203-216): shared ProxyAdmin owner only.
     Sibling `_authorizePortal` (L220-235) also requires the same SuperchainConfig (L227-228).
  2. OptimismPortal2.initialize (L273-303): accepts any nonzero lockbox; no `_ethLockbox.authorizedPortals(this)`.
     Sibling `migrateToSharedDisputeGame` checks it (L558-560). A portal initialised onto a lockbox that has not
     authorised it reverts on every value deposit and value withdrawal (`ETHLockbox_Unauthorized`).
  3. ETHLockbox.initialize (L94-109), AnchorStateRegistry.initialize (L94-133, `ethLockbox = _ethLockbox` L107),
     DelayedWETH.initialize (L58-64): no nonzero / code-length check on the pointer every pause, guardian and
     SuperchainConfig read now goes through. A zero or wrong pointer makes `paused()` revert: prove, finalize,
     `unlockETH`, bond withdrawal and Guardian actions stop; deposits (`lockETH`, no pause read) continue.
  4. Three independent copies of "the chain's lockbox" (`portal.ethLockbox`, `asr.ethLockbox`, `delayedWETH.ethLockbox`)
     and two of "the SuperchainConfig" (`lockbox.superchainConfig`, `systemConfig.superchainConfig`). Equality is
     enforced only at `_authorizePortal` time and by the off-chain validator (LOCKBOX-40, `-40`, `-50`).
  5. `_assertSharedProxyAdminOwner` reads the other contract's `proxyAdminOwner()` once, at authorisation; a later
     ProxyAdmin ownership transfer on either side is not re-checked.
  6. AnchorStateRegistry.setRespectedGameType (L158-165): any uint32, no factory lookup.
  7. SystemConfig.setFeature (L532-568): ETH_LOCKBOX and INTEROP are one-way; enabling ETH_LOCKBOX is now permitted
     while the global pause or the lockbox identifier is paused (only the portal identifier blocks it, L552).
     INTEROP can be set directly by the owner outside `migrate` (portal then accepts `migrateToSharedDisputeGame`).
  8. OPContractsManagerMigrator.migrate: the `OPTIMISM_PORTAL_INTEROP` dev-feature gate of v8 (v8 L116-119) is gone.
     v8 OPCM on mainnet (0x1951828C…, bitmap 0) could not migrate; v9 OPCM (0x2d91e6F2…) can, and
     `isPermittedMigrateSequence(OP SystemConfig)` is already true.
  9. OPContractsManagerV2._isPermittedInstruction (L347-376): `overrides.cfg.startingAnchorRoot` no longer passes;
     the key is still read in `_loadFullConfig` (L658), so only a direct ASR re-initialisation can move the anchor.
 10. A SystemConfig v4.3.0 paired with an ETHLockbox v1.3.1 recurses in `paused()` (SystemConfig L575 -> lockbox
     v8 L112 -> SystemConfig …); paired with a portal that has no lockbox it reverts. Safe only because the OPCM
     upgrades the three in one transaction.

Trigger: a ProxyAdmin-owner action (upgrade, migrate, or a direct ProxyAdmin call).

  Lane 09 PRIM-0904 measures what the real `migrate` does to the legacy contracts.
