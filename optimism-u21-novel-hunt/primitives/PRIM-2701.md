# PRIM-2701

PRIM-ID: PRIM-2701 — permissionless type-9 registration of arbitrary hash-consistent super-root preimages

Source contracts: `DisputeGameFactory`, `SuperFaultDisputeGame`, `AnchorStateRegistry` at op-contracts v9.0.0 (`ba6f...`).

Exact functions: `DisputeGameFactory.create`; `SuperFaultDisputeGame.initialize`; `AnchorStateRegistry.isGameProper`, `isGameRespected`, `isGameClaimValid`.

Trigger: Call `create(9, keccak256(extraData), extraData)` with the exact 0.08 ETH bond, a timestamp above the anchor, and structurally valid `0x01 || uint64 timestamp || (uint256 chainId || bytes32 root)+` data.

Attacker-controlled inputs: Creator, root claim, full super-root preimage, timestamp, ordered list of chain IDs and output roots.

Required state: Type 9 registered and currently respected; proposed timestamp greater than the shared anchor sequence; fresh factory UUID.

State mutation: Factory deploys/registers a CWIA clone, records the game, and deposits the bond into DelayedWETH. The clone snapshots `wasRespectedGameTypeWhenCreated = true`.

External calls: Factory to clone `initialize`; game to ASR `getAnchorRoot`/`respectedGameType`; game to VM oracle and DelayedWETH.

Cross-contract dependency: Portal proof/finalization later depends on the same game passing current-ASR registration and claim-validity checks.

Broken or weakened assumption: “Registered and respected” proves provenance/configuration, not semantic validity of the proposed root. The source explicitly warns that a proper game may have an incorrect root.

Candidate compositions: A decoder disagreement, incorrect defender resolution, or migration/domain mistake that turns a malformed registered game into a claim-valid one.

Evidence: `packages/contracts-bedrock/src/dispute/DisputeGameFactory.sol:142-225`; `.../SuperFaultDisputeGame.sol:251-341`; `.../AnchorStateRegistry.sol:273-353`; fork test `SuperRootPrimitivesTest.test_type9PermissionlessAndDuplicateChainIdsFirstWins` against block 26430859.

Confidence: High.

Reason it may be benign: This is the intended optimistic-fault-proof model. The reproduced malformed game was proper and respected but `IN_PROGRESS` and `isGameClaimValid == false`.
