# REJ-2505 — U21 itself corrupts or invalidates v8 proofs

## Idea

Changing portal/ASR implementations at block N might move proof storage or make every in-flight v8 proof unusable.

## Killing fact

The v8 and v9 portal storage-layout snapshots are byte-identical, and raw portal slots 0 and 50–63 were identical at N-1/N. A proof created at block 26429408 remains byte-identical at N and later. More strongly, withdrawal `0x6d7c…74af4` was proven under v8 at block 26377651, was unfinalized under v9 at N, and finalized successfully under v9 at block 26432294 (tx `0x734db3…77af`, event success true). Fork test `test_crossVersionProofFinalizedUnderV9` asserts this transition against deployed state.

ASR's U21 storage change adds the lockbox to unused packed bytes in slot 6; respected type/retirement/factory remain unchanged.

Disposition: Rejected; the later planned interop migration, not U21, is the documented invalidation boundary.
