# REJ-2102 — Incomplete or publicly re-runnable initialization

Hypothesis: Resetting the OZ-v4 byte left a proxy or implementation singleton callable by anyone after U21.

Killing fact: Every proxy's initialized byte was identical at N-1 and N: SuperchainConfig 2; SystemConfig/Portal/messenger/bridges 3; all others 1. Messenger was read at slot-0 byte offset 20, all others offset 0. Every distinct post implementation held `0xff` at its corresponding byte. Both direct proxy and direct singleton initializer calls revert in `U21ExecutionGuards.t.sol::test_proxyAndImplementationInitializersAreNotReRunnable`.

Source support: Every constructor calls `_disableInitializers`; exact ranges are listed in `mirror/lane-21/U21_INITIALIZER_CALLS.md`. Utils restores the real implementation after its post-init OZ-v5 check at `OPContractsManagerUtils.sol:374-386`.

Disposition: Rejected.
