# REJ-3102 — Unfinalized 90,000 ETH withdrawal can drain the lockbox

**Idea.** A real unfinalized plain withdrawal for 90,000 ETH looked like a high-value finalization target.

**Killing fact.** Fixture `pre-plain-invalid-90000eth.json` points to game `0xa5714949eb4CD7A9A13adF6854E10a444Ebe3619`, whose real `status()` is `1` (`CHALLENGER_WINS`). `checkWithdrawal` requires `anchorStateRegistry.isGameClaimValid` (`OptimismPortal2.sol:671-704`), so it cannot finalize. V9 permissionlessly deletes this proof, while deletion of the live valid fixture reverts; reproduced by `test/PortalStateInvariants.t.sol::test_deleteProvenWithdrawal_onlyDeletesPermanentlyInvalidProof`.

Deletion is gated to permanent challenger-win or ASR-blacklisted games at `OptimismPortal2.sol:707-727`.
