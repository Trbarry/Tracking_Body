# REJ-2204 — pause-identifier pivot silently unpauses a live chain

## Hypothesis

Changing Portal/SystemConfig/ASR/WETH pause resolution to the ETHLockbox identifier might ignore an active pause stored under the prior portal/global key.

## Killing facts

- At N-1, N, and 26433900, the shared SuperchainConfig mapping entries for global zero, each of four portals, and each of four lockboxes are raw zero and `paused(identifier) == false`. Exact derived slots are in `mirror/lane-22/PAUSE_AND_AUTHORIZATION_MAPPINGS.md`.
- All four SystemConfigs have ETH_LOCKBOX=true, INTEROP=false, CUSTOM_GAS_TOKEN=false at all checkpoints; every lockbox authorization entry for its portal is raw 1/true.
- v9 lockbox pause includes local-or-global state (`src/L1/ETHLockbox.sol:111-114`; `src/L1/SuperchainConfig.sol:139-154`). Portal, ASR, SystemConfig, and WETH all resolve through that live lockbox.
- The planned migrator checks current pause before changing a portal lockbox and repoints legacy ASR/WETH (`src/L1/opcm/OPContractsManagerMigrator.sol:488-555`); validator checks both legacy references (`src/L1/opcm/OPContractsManagerMigrationValidator.sol:612-660`).

## Disposition

Rejected for the deployed U21 transition. No active pause was lost and no dependency was zero/wrong.
