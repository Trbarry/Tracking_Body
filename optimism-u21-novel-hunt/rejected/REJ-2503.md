# REJ-2503 — front-run finalize by deleting, then force timer reset

## Idea

An attacker front-runs an honest finalization with deletion and forces the victim to re-prove/wait another maturity period.

## Killing fact

Immediately before deletion is allowed, the same proof fails `checkWithdrawal`: challenger-wins fails the defender-status requirement and blacklist fails properness through `isGameClaimValid` (`AnchorStateRegistry.sol:331-352`; portal `checkWithdrawal` at `OptimismPortal2.sol:671-705`). After deletion it fails earlier as `Unproven`; its ability to finalize is unchanged. Only `msg.sender` can overwrite/reset that submitter's timer during proving (`:480-487`), so the deleting attacker cannot re-prove under the victim's address.

Fork test `test_deleteFrontRunCannotChangeAnInvalidProofIntoAValidOne` observes `InvalidRootClaim` before deletion and `Unproven` after it.

Disposition: Rejected; no incremental delay caused by deletion.
