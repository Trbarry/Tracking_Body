# REJ-2203 — historical withdrawal proofs/finalizations corrupt across U21

## Hypothesis

v9 might read v8 `provenWithdrawals`, `proofSubmitters`, or `finalizedWithdrawals` entries with shifted base slots, reordered struct packing, or different nested-mapping semantics, blocking or replaying withdrawals.

## Killing facts

- The complete Portal storage snapshot is byte-identical in v7, v8, and v9. Base slots remain finalized=51, proven=57, submitters=60 (`snapshots/storageLayout/OptimismPortal2.json:37-42,86-119`).
- `ProvenWithdrawal` remains `{ IDisputeGame disputeGameProxy; uint64 timestamp; }`: v7 `src/L1/OptimismPortal2.sol:37-43`, v8 lines 38-44, v9 lines 39-45.
- Three recent pre-U21 `WithdrawalProven`/extension pairs were sampled from `https://ethereum-rpc.publicnode.com`. Their nested mapping words, dynamic-array length and element-0 words, decoded game/timestamp/submitter, and false finalized status are identical at blocks 26430858, 26430859, and 26433900.
- Three recent finalized hashes have raw mapping word 1 and getter `true` at all three checkpoints. Exact hashes/slots/events are in `mirror/lane-22/WITHDRAWAL_MAPPING_SAMPLES.json`.
- `StorageBoundaryTest.test_historicalWithdrawalMappingsAreBitExact` passes.

## Disposition

Rejected. Historical entries are read bit-for-bit under the same schema after U21.
