REJ-0606 — "U21 re-initialisation silently changed SystemConfig values that derivation consumes"

Idea: SystemConfig.initialize re-runs `_setBatcherHash`, `_setGasConfigEcotone`, `_setGasLimit` and re-emits ConfigUpdate; a chain whose scalars were stored only in legacy form would get zero fee scalars pushed to L2, or another slot would be clobbered.

Killing facts:
- Raw slots 0, 51, 101-108 and all named slots are bit-identical pre/post for all four SystemConfigs except OPCM_SLOT (0x1951828c… v8.0.1 -> 0x2d91e6f2… v9.0.3) (evidence/lane06/systemconfig-raw-slots-pre-vs-post.txt).
- The 12 SystemConfig ConfigUpdate logs in the U21 receipt (BATCHER, FEE_SCALARS, GAS_LIMIT x 4 chains) carry exactly the stored values; `scalar` == (1<<248)|blob<<32|base on every chain, so the re-emitted FEE_SCALARS is a no-op for op-node.
- eip1559 params (Ink: 0/0), operator fee (0 everywhere), minBaseFee (Soneium 12e6, Unichain 5e5), daFootprintGasScalar (0 everywhere) are not touched by initialize and are unchanged. Ink's 0/0 EIP-1559 params mean "never set" (node falls back to chain-config defaults) — not surprising given `_setEIP1559Params` is never called from initialize.
- The transient SystemConfig owner hop (owner -> ProxyAdmin -> owner) and DGF owner hop (PAO -> ProxyAdmin -> PAO) seen in the receipt are `__Ownable_init()` + `transferOwnership` inside one call; final owners unchanged.
