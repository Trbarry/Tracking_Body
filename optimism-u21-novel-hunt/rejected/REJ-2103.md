# REJ-2103 — Direct CALL to OPCM family steals deployed upgrade authority

Hypothesis: A permissionless caller directly invokes OPCMv2, Utils, Migrator, StorageSetter, or ProxyAdmin to modify a deployed chain.

Killing fact: Direct `upgradeSuperchain`, `upgrade`, and `migrate` calls to OPCMv2 revert with `OPContractsManagerV2_OnlyDelegateCall`. A fully shaped direct call to the standalone Migrator deploys only transient state and then fails at real ProxyAdmin authority; the transaction rollback leaves the SystemConfig implementation unchanged. Direct Utils and ProxyAdmin upgrade attempts likewise leave the real proxy unchanged. Direct StorageSetter calls write only the setter singleton's storage. All are reproduced by the six passing N-fork tests in `mirror/lane-21/tests/U21ExecutionGuards.t.sol`.

Source support: `_onlyDelegateCall` is `OPContractsManagerV2.sol:1073-1077`; ProxyAdmin mutation routes are `onlyOwner` at `ProxyAdmin.sol:152-181`; proxy-side guards are `ProxyAdminOwnedBase.sol:73-105`.

Disposition: Rejected for authority theft. Permissionless deterministic proxy deployment remains separately recorded as PRIM-2104.
