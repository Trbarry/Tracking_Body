REJ-0306 — Messages sent before U21 (old `baseGas`) are finalized but not recorded by the v9 L1CDM

Idea: v9 raises the L1 relay budgets (`L1_RELAY_RESERVED_GAS` 40k → 135k, `L1_RELAY_GAS_CHECK_BUFFER` 5k → 15k, `src/libraries/GasConstants.sol`). A withdrawal whose `gasLimit` was computed on L2 with the old constants (OP Mainnet L2CDM is still v2.2.1 at L2 block 159654093) could be finalized at minimum gas and leave the message in neither mapping.

Killing facts:
- Binary search for the lowest tx gas the portal accepts, then inspect the messenger (`test_G_*`):

| Real withdrawal | `_tx.gasLimit` | message `minGasLimit` | min tx gas | outcome at min gas |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| bridge ETH `0x01fa6374…e836` | 668,851 | 350,000 | 884,472 | relayed |
| 638 ETH `0xb16e5fc2…d612` | 313,808 | 0 | 518,078 | relayed |

- If the messenger did revert (out of gas, underflow of `gasleft() - 135_000`), v9 reverts the finalization (`OptimismPortal2.sol:651-653`), so the outcome is a retry with more gas, not a loss.
- The fork runs pre-Amsterdam rules; under EIP-8037 the minimum would rise but the same revert-and-retry rule applies. Not measured.
