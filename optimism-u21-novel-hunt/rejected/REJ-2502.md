# REJ-2502 — attacker bad proof overwrites or blocks victim good proof

## Idea

For 0.08 ETH, an attacker creates a respected type-9 game containing a victim withdrawal, proves it, and relies on that bad proof being selected instead of the victim's proof.

## Killing fact

Proving writes `provenWithdrawals[hash][msg.sender]`, not a withdrawal-global singleton (`OptimismPortal2.sol:480-487`). Finalization checks an explicitly supplied submitter; the convenience entry point supplies `msg.sender`, and the external entry point takes `_proofSubmitter` (`:496-498,577-607,671-705`). `proofSubmitters` order is never consumed on chain. A bad proof can neither overwrite the victim address's cell nor become an automatic selection.

`test_badSubmitterProofDoesNotBlockGoodSubmitterProof` deliberately puts a challenger-winning bad proof first, validates the good proof, deletes the bad proof, and validates the good proof again.

Disposition: Rejected; no direct blocking, delay, or theft.
