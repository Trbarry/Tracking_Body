# REJ-2303 — U21 left pre-upgrade ETH in the wrong custody contract

## Hypothesis

The implementation and pause-resolution upgrade might have switched custody while
pre-U21 liabilities remained backed in a portal or obsolete lockbox.

## Killing fact

At blocks 26430858, 26430859, and 26433900, OP portal balance is exactly zero, its
lockbox pointer remains `0x322b...4D43`, and that lockbox continues to authorize the
portal. All four upgraded portals have zero balance at all three points. The U21
receipt has no ETH custody or withdrawal-finalization event.

The OP lockbox's 4.204691597090892114 ETH N-1 -> N increase is fully explained by
an ordinary `ETHLocked` event at transaction index 341; U21 is index 358.

## Evidence

Passive `cast balance`, `ethLockbox()`, and `authorizedPortals()` reads are recorded
in `mirror/lane-23/STATE_BASELINE.md`; receipt/log filter records the unrelated
deposit transaction hash and exact amount.

## Conclusion

Killed. No upgrade-time value migration, orphaned portal balance, or pointer/auth
mismatch exists at the exact boundary.
