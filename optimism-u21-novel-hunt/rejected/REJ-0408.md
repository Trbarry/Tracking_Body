REJ-0408 - cheap gas-starvation of someone else's direct withdrawal (63/64 trick)

Idea: third party finalizes with just enough gas for the portal's check so the target runs out of gas, the
portal survives on its 1/64 and marks the withdrawal finalized.

Killing fact: `callWithMinGas` forwards all gas (SafeCall.sol:162); if the target burns it, the portal is left
with 1/64 of the gas at the CALL and needs ~13.2k (value) / ~3.7k (no value) to finish (l2Sender reset, feature
read, event, lockETH). Below that the outer transaction reverts (outcome 0) and nothing changes. Measured cold:
with `need = 500k, gasLimit = 100k, value 1 ETH` the outcomes are NotEnoughGas -> out-of-gas revert -> delivered,
with no undelivered window; the window only opens when the target still fails after receiving 830,645 gas
(231,294 without value; 430,114 / 301,794 for 1- / 2-level wrappers). The call-depth variant is dead since
EIP-150. (Fab.t.sol::test_gas_burner_*, Quant.t.sol::test_forced_failure_thresholds.) See PRIM-0402.
