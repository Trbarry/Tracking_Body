# REJ-1105 — "Proof isolation, withdrawal uniqueness or ETH conservation can be broken on the v9 portal by interleaving permissionless actions"

Ideas tried (all on fork `l1`, real bytecode, real fixtures):
1. Submitter B re-proving (replay of the recorded mainnet proof calldata), finalizing with an
   external proof, or `deleteProvenWithdrawal` changes submitter A's entry / ability to finalize.
2. A hash is delivered twice across `finalizeWithdrawalTransaction`,
   `finalizeWithdrawalTransactionExternalProof`, and `L1CDM.relayMessage` replay.
3. Some path leaves ETH in the portal, or lockbox + portal + messenger balances drift from
   Σdeposits − Σdelivered.
4. Re-entering portal / lockbox / messenger entry points from the withdrawal target.
5. `deleteProvenWithdrawal` usable against a proof whose game is not permanently invalid.

Killing facts:
- `test/poc/PortalInvariants.t.sol::invariant_all` — 24 runs × 40 calls (960 calls) and a second
  campaign 24 × 50 (1 200 calls), 0 failures. Handler mix and hit counts in
  `evidence/lane11/invariant-coverage*.txt`. Checked after every call:
  `portal.balance == Σ donateETH`; `L1CDM.balance == Σ value of failed-not-yet-relayed messages`;
  `lockbox.balance == initial + Σ deposits − Σ delivered − heldByMessenger`; per-hash finalize count
  ≤ 1; message delivered ≤ 1; finalized messenger withdrawal ⇒ successful ∨ failed; `l2Sender`
  reset; a successful finalize always had (proof by that submitter ∧ older than 7 d ∧
  `isGameClaimValid` ∧ not paused) BEFORE the call; `deleteProvenWithdrawal` succeeds ⇔ (proven ∧
  (game CHALLENGER_WINS ∨ blacklisted)); other submitters' entries bit-identical after every
  prove / delete; no forbidden re-entrant call (nested finalize from inside a withdrawal call,
  self-relay of the in-flight message, `lockbox.unlockETH`, `lockbox.lockETH`,
  `portal.migrateLiquidity`) ever succeeded.
- Differential D2 (`diff-*-D2-reprove.txt`, 224 keys, 0 differences v8/v9): B's replayed proof is
  stored under B with `timestamp = now`, A's entry unchanged, `numProofSubmitters` +1; A re-proving
  resets A's timer; a 1-bit-corrupted proof reverts ("MerkleTrie: invalid large internal hash");
  proving against the CHALLENGER_WINS game reverts `0xe29927ed`.
- Differential D6: `deleteProvenWithdrawal` on six valid/in-progress proofs →
  `0x42102de6 OptimismPortal_DisputeGameNotInvalidated`; on the forged proof (f9) → ok.
- Real multi-submitter fixtures f23–f28 (same hash proven by `0xa84f…` and `0xf7bd…` against
  different games): each finalizes with its own submitter in `FixtureE2E` (separate snapshots).
- Differential D3 (230 keys, deposits 0 … 120 001 bytes, EOA/contract, min / min−1 gas limit,
  receive(), creation flag): identical results, lockbox deltas and `TransactionDeposited` log
  hashes on v8 and v9; the only difference is `migrateLiquidity` from an EOA
  (`OptimismPortal_NotUsingInterop` → `ProxyAdminOwnedBase_NotProxyAdminOwner`, as per the diff).

What remains true (not a violation): `donateETH` permanently strands the donor's ETH in the portal
until the ProxyAdmin owner calls `migrateLiquidity`; a failed plain (non-messenger) withdrawal is
finalized with the value re-locked and no replay (documented, unchanged from v8); the nested
finalize-inside-replay of PRIM-1102.

Coverage limits: 2 160 handler calls in total; `finalize.ok` ≈ 5 % of calls; plain-fail and
replay-success paths are hit rarely by the random campaign (they are covered deterministically by
`InjectedGas` and `NestedReplay`); no second portal / shared-lockbox (interop) topology — the
single-portal lockbox of OP Mainnet only.
