REJ-0101: Re-running an initializer after U21 (by anyone, by the Safe, or by the ProxyAdmin)

Idea: v9 did not bump any `initVersion()` relative to v8, so maybe `initialize` is still open, or the OPCM's "zero the byte, re-run" pattern leaves a proxy at `_initialized == 0`.

Killing facts:
- Every proxy ends with `_initialized == initVersion()` (SuperchainConfig 2; SystemConfig, Portal, L1xDM, L1StandardBridge, L1ERC721Bridge 3; ETHLockbox, Factory, DGF, DelayedWETH, ASR 1), read from slot 0 at N and at 26433900 for all 41 proxies (`evidence/lane01-upgrades-decoded.json`, fields `slot0_n`, `slot0_post`).
- `reinitializer(initVersion())` requires `_initialized < version`; plus `_assertOnlyProxyAdminOrProxyAdminOwner()` (`ProxyAdminOwnedBase.sol:102-106`) on every initializer.
- Fork test `test/poc/lane01/Lane01_Negatives.t.sol::test_reinitialize_all_proxies_reverts`: the exact U21 initializer calldata replayed against all 41 proxies from an arbitrary address, from the Safe and from the proxy's ProxyAdmin reverts in all 123 cases.
- Re-initialisation needs `ProxyAdmin.upgrade(proxy, StorageSetter)`, i.e. the ProxyAdmin owner.
