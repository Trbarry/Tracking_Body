# REJ-3105 — Adversarial withdrawal target breaks custody or double-finalizes

**Idea.** Reverting, gas-burning, return-bomb, or reentrant target behavior could strand ETH in the portal, leave `l2Sender` set, bypass replay protection, or double-unlock from ETHLockbox.

**Killing fact.** On deployed v9 portal code, reverting and infinite-loop messenger replacements caused `OptimismPortal_L1CDMCallFailed`, restored lockbox and portal balances, and retained no finalization. A reentrant replacement could reenter inflow calls but both finalize entry points failed under the `l2Sender` guard (`OptimismPortal2.sol:591-595`); unauthorized lockbox lock/unlock also failed. The 36-action deterministic sweep and four 16x10 invariant passes preserved custody, portal-only-donation, messenger-outcome, and l2Sender restoration invariants. See `test/AdversarialTargets.t.sol` and `test/PortalHandlerInvariant.t.sol`.

The successful no-op messenger case is not a kill: it demonstrates PRIM-3106, but only after mocked code replacement and has no production attacker path.
