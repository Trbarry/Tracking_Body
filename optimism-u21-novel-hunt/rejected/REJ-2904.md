# REJ-2904 — Reuse one chain's withdrawal proof through another authorized portal

**Idea.** Once OP Mainnet and Unichain share a lockbox, prove one L2 withdrawal through both portals
and debit the common pool twice.

**Status:** Rejected.

**Exact killing facts:**

- For a super game, the portal selects
  `disputeGameProxy.rootClaimByChainId(systemConfig.l2ChainId())` before checking the output-root
  proof (`OptimismPortal2.sol:437-449`). The withdrawal trie proof is therefore against that
  portal's chain-specific message-passer storage root (`OptimismPortal2.sol:452-478`).
- Super-game implementations search the committed super-root tuple list for the requested chain ID
  and revert if absent (`SuperPermissionedDisputeGame.sol:80-89` and
  `SuperFaultDisputeGame.sol:806-818`).
- Migration rejects zero, duplicate, or unsorted chain IDs specifically because duplicates would
  permit cross-portal finalization (`OPContractsManagerMigrator.sol:287-327`).
- Live OP Mainnet and Unichain IDs are 10 and 130, in the required ascending order. Both return
  `isPermittedMigrateSequence = true`.

Identical withdrawal hashes can validly exist on both chains, but then two independent L2 burns and
two chain-specific inclusion proofs exist. No one-burn/two-unlock sequence was found.
