# REJ-3201 — Failed L1 messenger withdrawal cannot leave partial finalization

Hypothesis: v9 marks a withdrawal finalized and unlocks ETH before its target call, then checks the low-level return only afterward; a failed L1 messenger relay might therefore consume the withdrawal or leak partial target state.

Killing fact: `OptimismPortal2.finalizeWithdrawalTransactionExternalProof` sets `finalizedWithdrawals`, calls `unlockETH`, sets `l2Sender`, and performs `SafeCall.callWithMinGas` at v9 `OptimismPortal2.sol:609-630`; a failed call whose L2 sender is the messenger reverts at `:651-653`. That revert atomically rolls back the portal write, lockbox transfer, and nested external-call state. The withdrawal remains retryable. Non-messenger calls retain the longstanding explicit `success` event/refund behavior.

Disposition: Rejected; no persistent partial mutation or permanent freeze sequence found.
