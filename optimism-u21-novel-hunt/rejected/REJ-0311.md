REJ-0311 — Deposit mints more on L2 than is locked on L1

Idea: `depositTransaction` takes both `msg.value` and `_value`; a mismatch, a zero-value edge, or the `receive()` path might mint without locking.

Killing facts:
- `opaqueData = abi.encodePacked(msg.value, _value, …)` (`OptimismPortal2.sol:790`): the mint field is `msg.value`, the same amount passed to `lockETH` (`:758`).
- `test_A_deposit_valueGoesToLockbox_mintEqualsLocked`: `msg.value = 3 ETH`, `_value = 1 ETH` → `ETHLocked = 3 ETH`, mint field 3 ETH, lockbox +3 ETH, portal 0.
- `test_A_deposit_valueExceedingMsgValue_isOnlyAnL2Concern`: `msg.value = 1 wei`, `_value = 1,000,000 ETH` → lockbox +1 wei.
- Zero-value deposit emits no `ETHLocked`; `receive()` locks the full amount.
- On-chain since U21: ΣETHLocked == Σmint fields on all four chains (95 deposits on OP).
