# REJ-3206 — SafeCall new-account gas check causes current permanent withdrawal loss

Hypothesis: the new 183,600-gas account-creation surcharge in `SafeCall.callWithMinGas` causes a valid value-bearing withdrawal to be finalized without executing its target.

Killing fact: v9 adds the surcharge only to the pre-call sufficiency test when value is nonzero and `EXTCODEHASH` is zero (`SafeCall.sol:9,120-131`). On the pinned pre-Amsterdam fork this is conservative over-reservation, not underfunding. For L2-messenger withdrawals, a failed target call causes the portal to revert (`OptimismPortal2.sol:651-653`), rolling back finalization and allowing a higher-gas retry. A future Amsterdam-state-gas bug cannot honestly be demonstrated on this deployed fork.

Disposition: Rejected for current mainnet impact; cross-version gas composition retained as PRIM-3202.
