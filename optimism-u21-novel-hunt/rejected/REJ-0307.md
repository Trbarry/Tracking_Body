REJ-0307 — Custom-gas-token mode lets value be in the portal and counted by the lockbox

Idea: v9 requires a lockbox on every chain (`_assertValidLockboxState`, `OptimismPortal2.sol:839-843`), CGT chains included, so `_isUsingLockbox()` is true there. Some path might still lock or unlock ETH.

Killing facts (code only; none of the four upgraded chains has `CUSTOM_GAS_TOKEN`):
- `depositTransaction` reverts on `msg.value > 0` before the lock (`:752-754`); prove and finalize revert on `_tx.value > 0` (`:407-409`, `:587-589`); `migrateLiquidity` reverts (`:507`). The relock branch needs `_tx.value > 0`.
- The only ETH that can enter a CGT portal is `donateETH` / forced ETH, which no path spends.
- CGT cannot be enabled on upgrade (`OPContractsManagerV2.sol:1005-1007`) and the Migrator refuses CGT chains (`OPContractsManagerMigrator.sol:484-486`).
- A non-PAO `setFeature` call reverts (`test_B_portal_callerRestrictions`).
