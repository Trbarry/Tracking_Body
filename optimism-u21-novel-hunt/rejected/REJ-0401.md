REJ-0401 - "relayMessage returns to the portal without delivering AND without recording the message"

Idea: find an exit of `relayMessage` that is not a revert and writes neither `successfulMessages` nor
`failedMessages` (early return, version/nonce branch, value mismatch, blocked-target branch, estimation branch).
Then the v9 portal would finalize a messenger withdrawal with no replay path (invariant A).

Killing facts (op-v9.0.0 src/universal/CrossDomainMessenger.sol):
- :222, :225, :231, :245, :247, :250, :254 are `require`s and :242-243 are `assert`s -> revert -> portal reverts
  `OptimismPortal_L1CDMCallFailed` (OptimismPortal2.sol:651) because the sender is the L2CDM.
- the only `return` (:281) is preceded by `failedMessages[versionedHash] = true` (:269).
- after the target call either :292 (`successfulMessages`) or :295 (`failedMessages`) is written.
- the ESTIMATION_ADDRESS branches (:277, :303) revert.
- blocked-target branch: in v9 it is a `require` (:250), it does not store `failedMessages`.
- portal tail after the call (l2Sender reset, event) running out of gas reverts the whole transaction, including
  the messenger writes (single tx) - observed as outcome 0 in the sweeps.
Fork evidence: outcome code 6 ("finalized, neither mapping set") never occurs in any sweep
(GasSweep.t.sol, Fab2.t.sol::test_big_*, CrossVersion.t.sol::test_v8_sweep), from 100k gas upward in 1,000-gas
steps, for real ETH / ERC20 withdrawals and synthetic 20 KB-500 KB messages.
