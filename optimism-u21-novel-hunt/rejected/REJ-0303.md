REJ-0303 — ETH left in the portal is spent by a later withdrawal, or is taken by a non-admin

Idea: leave ETH in the portal (donation from inside a withdrawal, forced ETH) and have a later finalization pay it out, or pull it with `migrateLiquidity`.

Killing facts:
- Finalization unlocks exactly `_tx.value` (`OptimismPortal2.sol:614`) and forwards exactly `_tx.value` (`:627`). The only `address(this).balance` read in the portal is `migrateLiquidity` (`:510`), which is PAO-only (`:505`).
- `test_D_direct_reentrantTarget_matrix_and_strandedDonation`: 20 ETH donated mid-withdrawal stays at 20 ETH after a later 638 ETH finalization; attacker `migrateLiquidity()` reverts `ProxyAdminOwnedBase_NotProxyAdminOwner`; `vm.prank(PAO)` sweep moves exactly 20 ETH to the lockbox.
- `ETH_LOCKBOX` cannot be disabled in v9 (`SystemConfig.sol:543-547`; `test_B_portal_callerRestrictions`), so `_isUsingLockbox()` cannot flip between the two reads in one finalization.
- Portal balance is 0 on OP, Ink, Soneium, Unichain both at 26430858 and 26433900.
