# REJ-2402 — Double finalization through alternate proof submitters or games

## Idea

Prove one withdrawal under multiple submitters/games, then use `finalizeWithdrawalTransactionExternalProof` once per record.

## Killing fact

Proofs are per `(withdrawalHash,submitter)`, but execution is gated by and commits `finalizedWithdrawals[withdrawalHash]` (`OptimismPortal2.sol:603-610,671-679`). The bit is not keyed by submitter or game. Two-real-game fork test executed the target once and reverted the alternate proof with `OptimismPortal_AlreadyFinalized` (`mirror/lane-24/tests/WithdrawalFinalization24.t.sol:478-495`). Pinned source test confirms the intended behavior (`test/L1/OptimismPortal2.t.sol:2357-2403`).
