REJ-0310 — Pause asymmetry strands or duplicates value

Idea: deposits are accepted while unlocks are blocked; a mismatch between the portal's and the lockbox's pause views could finalize without paying, or pay while "paused".

Killing facts:
- Portal and lockbox read the same value: `portal.paused()` is `ethLockbox.paused()` (`OptimismPortal2.sol:306-308`), and `unlockETH` checks the same function (`ETHLockbox.sol:159`). A finalization that passes `:584` cannot hit `ETHLockbox_Paused` in the same tx.
- `test_E_…`: with the lockbox key paused, finalize reverts `OptimismPortal_CallPaused`, a 1 ETH deposit still locks 1 ETH, and nothing leaves. With only the portal address paused nothing is paused and a real withdrawal finalizes normally.
- Deposits during a pause create a normal liability backed by the locked ETH.
