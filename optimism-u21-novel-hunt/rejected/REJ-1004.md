REJ-1004 — "A permissionless finalizer can destroy or permanently block an in-flight messenger withdrawal by choosing the gas" (old L2 budget vs new L1 reserve; relayMessage revert -> `OptimismPortal_L1CDMCallFailed`)

Idea A: with the 105k deficit of PRIM-1001, finalize with exactly the portal minimum so that `relayMessage`
passes `hasMinGas` and then runs out of gas -> message in neither mapping, withdrawal finalized.
Idea B: make `relayMessage` revert on every attempt so the withdrawal can never be finalized (new revert at
`OptimismPortal2.sol:651-653`).

Killing facts:
- A: `OptimismPortal2.sol:651-653` reverts the whole finalization when the call fails and `_tx.sender` is the
  L2CDM, so "finalized without record" is unreachable for messenger withdrawals. Fork scan
  (`test/poc/lane10/MessengerGasCrossVersion.t.sol`, real contracts at 26433900): 0 such outcomes over every
  sampled gas value; the only outcomes are revert, relayed, or `failedMessages` recorded.
- A (today's rules): the relay does not even fail at the minimum: old-budget 1 ETH to a non-existent account is
  relayed at the first gas value that does not revert (454,000); a target with minGas 500,000 receives >= 666,269.
- A (replay): `failedMessages` is only set on the portal path after `assert(msg.value == _value)`
  (`CrossDomainMessenger.sol:239-242`), so the ETH for every replayable message is in the L1CDM. L1CDM, L1StandardBridge
  and portal ETH balances are 0 at 26433900.
- B: `relayMessage` reverts only for: pause (portal checks first), version >= 2, legacy v0 already relayed, unsafe
  target (L1CDM or portal), already-successful hash, `msg.value != _value`, ESTIMATION origin, or out of gas. All but the
  last are fixed by the message itself, which only the L2CDM can author; out of gas is cured by more gas. After the
  CALL the frame needs ~25k (two SSTOREs + log) against a 135,000 reserve minus at most 36,600 of CALL charges
  under today's rules; the message is copied to memory before the check (`bytes memory _message`, `:215`), and
  `SafeCall.call` copies no returndata.
- Edge that does exist but is self-inflicted: a message whose `minGasLimit` puts the required gas within ~105k of the
  16,777,216 per-transaction cap (EIP-7825) was relayable under 2.11.1 and is not under 2.13.0.

Residual (not a U21-contract issue, not reproducible here): chains still on the pre-U21 messenger + portal when
EIP-8037 activates — see PRIM-1001.
