# REJ-3205 — Re-migration or chain-ID alias bypass

Hypothesis: repeat `migrate`, duplicate/zero chain IDs, or an out-of-order chain list can corrupt a shared lockbox/DGF or let one super-root prove against multiple portals.

Killing fact: the migrator checks common ProxyAdmin owner and SuperchainConfig, rejects an already-set INTEROP flag, and enforces nonzero/distinct/ascending L2 chain IDs before any deployment or mutation (`OPContractsManagerMigrator.sol:291-327`). `SystemConfig.setFeature` makes INTEROP irreversible (`SystemConfig.sol:557-567`). OPCMv2 separately enforces a permitted migrate sequence for every chain before delegatecall (`OPContractsManagerV2.sol:293-303`). On all four current chains INTEROP is false and an unprivileged direct portal migration fails at the feature gate before authorization (`NegativeSpace.t.sol::test_migrateToSharedDisputeGame_featureGateRunsBeforeAuthorization`).

Disposition: Rejected for permissionless exploitation; migration remains privileged and prechecked.
