REJ-0807 — "Copying the payload to memory before the gas check makes large in-flight messages that v8 could
finalize impossible to finalize on v9"

Killing facts
1. Total memory use is the same (v8 copied the payload at the `SafeCall.call` site, v9 at ABI decode); measured
   pre-check cost for 100 kB / 300 kB is 473,165 / 1,909,203 on v9 vs 471,575 / 1,907,613 on v8.
2. Minimum direct-transaction gas to finalize is *lower* on v9: 100 kB live 6,335,742 vs 6,338,854; 300 kB live
   15,525,061 vs 15,528,173.
3. What changes is the outcome when the target consumes its gas: v8 finalizes with no record (permanent loss,
   row 14 of the v8 table), v9 records a replayable failure.
4. Messages too large for any L1 transaction (portal check `gasLimit*64/63 + 40,000` above the per-tx gas cap) were
   never finalizable on either version.
Status: dead — v9 is strictly better here.
