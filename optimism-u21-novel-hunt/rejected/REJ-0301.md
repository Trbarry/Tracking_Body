REJ-0301 — Double unlock of one withdrawal (re-entrancy, second submitter, replay)

Idea: get `ETHLockbox.unlockETH(_tx.value)` to run twice for one withdrawal hash: nested `finalize…` from the target, a second proof submitter, or a retry after a failed call.

Killing facts:
- `finalizedWithdrawals[hash] = true` is written at `OptimismPortal2.sol:610`, before `unlockETH` (`:614`) and before the target call (`:627`); `checkWithdrawal` rejects on it first (`:677`). It is keyed by hash, not by submitter.
- Nested finalize of any withdrawal reverts `OptimismPortal_NoReentrancy` (`:594-596`): observed selector `0xdfeaaeb8` from an etched recipient in `test_C_…reentrancyMatrix` and from an etched direct target in `test_D_…matrix…`.
- Second call after success reverts `OptimismPortal_AlreadyFinalized` (`test_B_finalize_guards`, `test_C_finalize_real638ETH_exactOnce`).
- 256 random sequences of deposit / donate / finalize over five real ready withdrawals: each finalizes exactly once and Δlockbox == Σdeposits − Σfinalized values at every step (`testFuzz_F_sequenceConservation`).
