REJ-0308 — A portal unlocks ETH that backs another chain

Idea: a portal authorized on a lockbox it does not belong to could drain it with its own (cheaper to forge) withdrawals.

Killing facts: at 26433900 each of the four lockboxes returns `authorizedPortals(own portal) = true` and `false` for the other three portals and for Base's portal. Authorization is PAO-only and requires the same PAO and the same SuperchainConfig (`ETHLockbox.sol:124-130`, `:220-235`). All lock/unlock events since U21 carry the lockbox's own portal as topic.

Open: the full historical `PortalAuthorized` / `LockboxAuthorized` sets were not enumerated (archive log queries unavailable).
