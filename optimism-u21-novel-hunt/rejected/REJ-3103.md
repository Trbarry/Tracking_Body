# REJ-3103 — Pause identifier mismatch permits custody exit

**Idea.** U21 rewiring from portal to lockbox pause identifier might leave one value-moving route unpaused and permit withdrawals during an emergency pause.

**Killing fact.** With the real guardian pranked solely to create `pause(lockbox)`, portal, lockbox, SystemConfig, and L1CDM all reported paused. Proving, both finalization routing and lockbox unlock reverted. No outflow succeeded. Test: `test/PortalStateInvariants.t.sol::test_pauseBlocksProofsFinalizationAndUnlocks_butKeepsInflowsOpen`.

Deposits, messenger sends, receive, and donations remain open, but they only increased custody by exactly 4 ETH. This is intentional: `ETHLockbox.sol:158` says unlocks are blocked while locks are not. Thus the blanket “all value-moving calls revert” invariant is false, but no drain or pause bypass exists.
