# REJ-1103 — "SafeCall.NEW_ACCOUNT_STATE_GAS (pre-positioned Amsterdam constant) freezes value withdrawals to empty accounts under today's gas rules"

Idea: v9 `SafeCall.callWithMinGas` adds 183 600 to the required gas when
`_value != 0 && _target.codehash == 0` (SafeCall.sol:9,131-134) although EIP-8037 is not active on
L1. A withdrawal whose `gasLimit` sits just under the EIP-7825 tx gas cap could become
unfinalizable, or a third party could exploit the extra requirement.

Killing facts (`evidence/lane11/injected-gas-{l1pre,l1}-P.json`, HARNESS-injected plain withdrawals,
real bytecode, cold access):

| plain withdrawal, 1 ETH | min gas v8 | min gas v9 | Δ |
|---|---|---|---|
| empty account, gasLimit 0 | 204 243 | 374 151 | **+169 908** |
| empty account, gasLimit 100 000 | 307 444 | 477 351 | **+169 907** |
| funded EOA (1 wei), gasLimit 0 | 204 243 | 187 647 | −16 596 |
| contract, gasLimit 100 000 | 307 444 | 290 847 | −16 597 |
| empty account, value 0, gasLimit 0 | 177 065 | 171 413 | −5 652 |

- +169 908 = +183 600 (constant) + ≈2 900 (cold EXTCODEHASH path) − 16 596 (cheaper pause path).
  The extra gas is only REQUIRED, not consumed: the call always ends `FINALIZED_PLAIN_OK`, the target
  receives exactly the value, lockbox delta = −value (per-probe checks, 0 violations).
- The condition is removable by anyone: sending 1 wei to the target makes `codehash != 0` (the
  "funded EOA" row), so even a withdrawal within 183 600 gas of the tx gas cap is not frozen.
- Through the messenger the inner call does not use `callWithMinGas`: value → empty account relays at
  the portal floor on both forks (S1 #21,#23,#40,#42; Δ = −16 596).
- No class change on any probe (plain set: 7 645 probes on v9, 7 629 on v8; only the boundary moves).

Not locally reproducible: behaviour under Amsterdam (EIP-8037) — whether 135 000 reserved in
`relayMessage` survives a value CALL to a new account (183 600 state gas charged in the messenger
frame). GasConstants.sol:36-43 claims "baseGas absorbs it"; that is an argument from the EIP text
for another lane, not something this harness can measure.
