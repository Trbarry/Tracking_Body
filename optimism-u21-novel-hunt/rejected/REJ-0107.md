REJ-0107: The pause-identifier consolidation onto the ETHLockbox silently drops an active pause

Idea: before U21 the identifier was the lockbox only when ETH_LOCKBOX was enabled, otherwise the portal; after U21 everything resolves through `ETHLockbox.paused()` -> `SuperchainConfig.isLocalOrGlobalPaused(lockbox)`. A pause recorded under a portal address would stop applying.

Killing facts: at N-1 `ETH_LOCKBOX` was already true on all four chains (identifier already the lockbox), and `SuperchainConfig.expiration(x) == 0` for address(0), the four portals and the four lockboxes at N-1 and at 26433900. `SystemConfig.setFeature` additionally refuses to enable ETH_LOCKBOX while the portal identifier is paused (`SystemConfig.sol`, setFeature ETH_LOCKBOX branch). Nothing was paused, nothing could be dropped.
