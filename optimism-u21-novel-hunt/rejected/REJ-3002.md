# REJ-3002 — pre-meter Lockbox call enables deposit reentrancy or persistent partial effects

Hypothesis: `depositTransaction` calls Lockbox before target/gas/calldata validation and before `_metered`, leaving ETH or metering inconsistent.

Killing facts:

- Ordering is real (`ResourceMetering.sol:66-75`; `OptimismPortal2.sol:741-795`) but identical in v8 (`v8 OptimismPortal2.sol:684-738`) and feature was already true at N−1.
- `ETHLockbox.lockETH` only authenticates Portal and emits; it makes no callback (`ETHLockbox.sol:142-151`).
- Direct attacker call reverts. A deliberately invalid creation deposit rolls back Lockbox balance and all meter fields. A valid 1 ETH deposit raises Lockbox balance by 1 ETH and `prevBoughtGas` by 100,000 atomically (`Lane30ResourceMeteringTest`, three passing tests).

Conclusion: rejected; current EVM atomicity and no-callback Lockbox kill the path.
