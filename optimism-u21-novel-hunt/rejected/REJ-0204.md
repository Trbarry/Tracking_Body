REJ-0204 — `provenWithdrawals` / `proofSubmitters` / `finalizedWithdrawals` entries written under v8 are decoded differently by v9

Idea: struct or slot drift (e.g. `ProvenWithdrawal` gaining a field, or the mapping moving to the legacy slot 52) would
let old proofs be read with a shifted game address / timestamp (instant finalisation or replay of finalised withdrawals).

Killing facts:
- Layout identical in v7/v8/v9: slot 51, 57, 60; struct `{IDisputeGame disputeGameProxy; uint64 timestamp}` unchanged
  (src/L1/OptimismPortal2.sol:42-45, 62, 95, 116).
- 21 real entries (12 OP, 1 Ink, 4 Soneium, 4 Unichain; evidence/lane02/withdrawal_mapping_check.json): raw slots and
  getters byte-identical at N-1 (v8 impl `0x1005217a..`) and N (v9 impl `0xb20b23af..`).
- 4 OP Mainnet withdrawals proven under v8 (2026-10-20 .. 2026-11-09) passed `checkWithdrawal` at N-1 and N and were
  finalised under v9 at blocks 26430910, 26431124, 26432294, 26433013; `finalizedWithdrawals` 0 -> 1, then
  `OptimismPortal_AlreadyFinalized`.
- `test_entriesProvenUnderV8ReadIdenticallyUnderV9` PASS (raw word == `timestamp << 160 | game`).
U21 itself invalidated no proof (same ASR, same DGF, `retirementTimestamp` untouched).
