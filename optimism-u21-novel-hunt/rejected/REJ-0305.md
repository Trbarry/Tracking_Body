REJ-0305 — U21 left a pre-upgrade object's value in the wrong contract

Idea: the v9 upgrade path can deploy a lockbox and sweep the portal (`OPContractsManagerV2.sol:895-897`). A deposit in flight, a proven withdrawal, or ETH left by a failed finalization might end up counted in the wrong place.

Killing facts (cast reads at 26430858 and 26433900, `evidence/lane03/reconcile_u21_window.json`, U21 receipt):
- All four chains already had `ETH_LOCKBOX = true` and a lockbox before U21; portal, L1CDM and L1StandardBridge balances are 0 before and after. `wasUsingEthLockbox` was true, so `migrateLiquidity()` did not run: the receipt has no `ETHMigrated`, `LiquidityMigrated` or `LiquidityReceived`, only `PortalAuthorized(own portal)` per lockbox.
- From the U21 block to the fork block, Δlockbox equals ΣETHLocked − ΣETHUnlocked exactly on all four chains (OP +124.761729954981417603 ETH: 16 locks, 3 unlocks; Unichain +43.810927375207071116 ETH; Ink and Soneium 0), and ΣETHLocked equals the sum of deposit mint fields. No event came from a foreign portal.
- Withdrawals proven before U21 finalize after it with the expected flow (`test_C_finalize_real638ETH_exactOnce`, proven at 1794291647, U21 at 1794871439).
- Lockbox slot 3 (`superchainConfig`, new in v2.0.0) was 0 before and `0x9570…4a4C` after on all four; `paused()` resolves.
