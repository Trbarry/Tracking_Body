# REJ-2507 — retired/unregistered proof cannot be deleted and permanently freezes withdrawal

## Idea

Deletion omits other permanent invalidity conditions. Retirement and the planned registry swap can leave an in-progress/defender proof stored but undeletable.

## Killing fact

The omission is confirmed, but the mapping is not a uniqueness lock. The same submitter may re-prove and overwrite its cell; another submitter has an independent cell (`OptimismPortal2.sol:480-487`). Finalization explicitly selects a current cell. After migration, a new proof against a shared game overwrites the old proof and simply appends another discovery entry. `test_plannedMigrationMechanismRejectsAllOldFactoryProofsButDoesNotDeleteThem` confirms that the old bytes persist and fail validation, and `test_retirementAloneDoesNotAuthorizeDeletion` confirms the narrower delete gate.

The migration invalidation and need to re-prove are explicitly documented at `OPContractsManagerV2.sol:280-287` and in the U21 notice.

Disposition: Rejected; stale state/cleanup incompleteness without fund loss or permanent freeze.
