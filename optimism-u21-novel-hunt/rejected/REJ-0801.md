REJ-0801 — "Live L2CDM budgets (old constants) are too small for the v9 L1 relay, so in-flight / future messenger
withdrawals are permanently unfinalizable"

Idea: L2CDM 2.2.1 `baseGas` charges 40,000 + 5,000 for the L1 reserve/buffer, L1CDM 2.13.0 needs 135,000 + 15,000
and copies the payload before the check; the portal now reverts on messenger failure.

Killing facts
1. `SafeCall.callWithMinGas` forwards `gas()` (SafeCall.sol:162); `_tx.gasLimit` is only the floor of the check at
   SafeCall.sol:131-132. More gas in the L1 transaction always reaches `relayMessage`.
2. At the portal minimum the L1CDM proxy is entered with `gasLimit + 32,634…36,733` gas and spends ~40.3k before the
   check, so the margin over `ceil(64m/63) + 190,000` is +87k…+114k for every bridge-style message
   (measure_tables.md rows 0-6) and stays positive up to `minGasLimit` 5.76M (old budget) / 7.32M (live budget).
3. Above that band the check fails and the message is *recorded failed and returns* (CrossDomainMessenger.sol:269-281):
   portal finalizes, anyone replays. Real tx: validate_real_tx.jsonl case 20.
4. Payload size: budget 16-40 gas/byte vs measured pre-check ~3.1 gas/byte + 9.6e-6*len^2 -> negative only beyond
   ~1.3 MB, which no L1 transaction can carry (300 kB already costs 15.5M gas).
5. Real population: all 38 withdrawals proven in the 5k blocks before U21 finalize on the post-U21 fork with the
   same outcome as on the pre-U21 fork, at the minimum gas (inflight_l1.log vs inflight_l1pre.log).
Status: dead. Residue recorded as PRIM-0801.
