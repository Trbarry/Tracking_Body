# REJ-2104 — Successful U21 leaves a partial four-chain bundle

Hypothesis: A late-chain failure can commit earlier chain upgrades, or a Safe-level success can mask partial state.

Killing fact: The decoded aggregate has five calls and `allowFailure=false` for every entry. A failing inner call reverts the aggregate delegatecall, rolling back all earlier calls; the Safe may consume its nonce and emit failure at its own layer, but cannot retain a partial aggregate state. The executed transaction emitted Safe `ExecutionSuccess`, 307 fully attributed logs, and a coherent 47-slot final diff with no unmapped slot. See `decoded-transaction.json`, `decoded-logs.json`, and `attributed-state-diff.json`.

Ordering was shared SuperchainConfig, then OP, Ink, Soneium, Unichain. Each chain's internal order is fixed at `OPContractsManagerV2.sol:831-996`. StorageSetter transitions are intra-transaction and expose no block boundary.

Disposition: Rejected.
