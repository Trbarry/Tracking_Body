REJ-0110: Abusing the permissionless `OPContractsManagerV2.deploy()` (authority held by the OPCM, or CREATE2 collisions with live or planned deployments)

Idea: `deploy()` has no `_onlyDelegateCall`; during it the OPCM is owner of a ProxyAdmin and a DisputeGameFactory. Could a caller obtain authority over an existing chain, or pre-occupy an address a governance action will deploy to?

Killing facts:
- In `deploy()` the OPCM only owns the ProxyAdmin/DGF it has just created for the caller's new chain and hands them to `_cfg.proxyAdminOwner` at the end (`OPContractsManagerV2.sol:1018-1024`); it never becomes owner of anything pre-existing, and the contract has no storage.
- Addresses: `deploy()` deploys from the OPCM address with salt `keccak(l2ChainId, bytes20(msg.sender) ++ saltMixer, name)` (line 222); `upgrade()`/`migrate()` deploy from the Safe (delegatecall context) with unrelated salts. Different deployer, so no collision with governance deployments. Two callers can only collide if they share `msg.sender` (e.g. both go through the same public forwarder) - a foot-gun for new-chain deployers, no effect on live chains.
- A freshly deployed fake chain cannot attach to a real one: `ETHLockbox.authorizePortal/authorizeLockbox` are ProxyAdmin-owner only and check `_assertSharedProxyAdminOwner`; the real messengers/bridges only trust their own portal.
