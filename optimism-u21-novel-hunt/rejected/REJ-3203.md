# REJ-3203 — Lockbox sibling path bypass

Hypothesis: the checked `lockETH` path can be bypassed through `receiveLiquidity`, `unlockETH`, an old lockbox, or authorization functions.

Killing fact: deployed `lockETH` requires an authorized portal (`ETHLockbox.sol:144-150`); `receiveLiquidity` requires an authorized lockbox (`:133-139`); `unlockETH` checks pause, authorized portal, balance, and that the portal is in a withdrawal context (`:157-174`). `authorizePortal`, `authorizeLockbox`, and `migrateLiquidity` are ProxyAdmin-owner-only (`:124-129,184-213`) and shared-owner/config checks run before authorization mutation. Fork calls from an unprivileged address reverted with `ETHLockbox_Unauthorized` or `ProxyAdminOwnedBase_NotProxyAdminOwner` in two NegativeSpace tests.

`donateETH` and forced ETH do bypass authorization, but only move attacker-funded ETH and do not mint claims; retained as PRIM-3201, not a loss-of-funds candidate.
