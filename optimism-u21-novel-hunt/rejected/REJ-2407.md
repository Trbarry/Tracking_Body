# REJ-2407 — Current ASR/retirement/blacklist change permanently strands old proofs

## Idea

Finalization validates a previously created proof through the current `anchorStateRegistry`. Migration, a higher retirement timestamp, blacklist, or pause can invalidate it after maturity.

## Killing fact

The dynamic invalidation is real (`OptimismPortal2.sol:671-704`; `AnchorStateRegistry.sol:286-353`), but it invalidates the proof record, not the L2 withdrawal or its hash. `proveWithdrawalTransaction` expressly allows the same submitter/hash to re-prove and overwrite/reset the record (`OptimismPortal2.sol:480-487`). Migration-invalidates-old-proofs/re-prove is documented U21 behavior. The real pre-U21 proof remained byte-for-byte present across N, demonstrating no storage loss (`REAL_WITHDRAWAL_EVIDENCE.md`).

A privileged migration could select a lockbox address equal to a withdrawal target or otherwise misconfigure dependencies, but that is privileged/operator-controlled and outside a permissionless finding.
