# REJ-3204 — Permissionless violation of validator-only configuration invariants

Hypothesis: after StandardValidator passes, a permissionless caller can clear ETH_LOCKBOX/INTEROP, zero the respected game bond, change registry/game type, or break the shared-owner bindings.

Killing fact: deployed unauthorized calls to `SystemConfig.setFeature`, `AnchorStateRegistry.setRespectedGameType`, and `DisputeGameFactory.setInitBond` revert respectively with `ProxyAdminOwnedBase_NotProxyAdminOrProxyAdminOwner`, `AnchorStateRegistry_Unauthorized`, and `Ownable: caller is not the owner` (`NegativeSpace.t.sol::test_configurationValidatorsCannotBeViolatedPermissionlessly`). All four chains have exact portal/ASR/WETH/lockbox bindings and a shared SuperchainConfig at block 26433900. Type 9 bonds are 0.08 ETH; Soneium's respected permissioned type 5 intentionally has zero bond.

Disposition: Rejected except the inherently mutable balance assertions isolated as PRIM-3201.
