# REJ-2401 — Third party gas-caps a direct contract withdrawal into finalized non-delivery

## Idea

Use `finalizeWithdrawalTransactionExternalProof` with another account's mature proof and an outer gas cap that passes the withdrawal's encoded minimum but leaves the target too little gas. A non-messenger failure remains globally finalized and value is re-locked, eliminating the Portal replay path.

## Reproduction

Deployed v6.1.0 Portal on the post-U21 fork: 1 ETH, encoded minimum 30,000, target requirement 260,000. At outer cap 350,000, the outer call succeeded, target call reverted, target received zero, value returned to the Lockbox, and the hash became finalized. With adequate outer gas, the equivalent withdrawal delivered. See `mirror/lane-24/tests/WithdrawalFinalization24.t.sol:275-335`.

## Exact killing facts

1. The behavior is explicit: only the L2 messenger sender gets failure rollback; “all other withdrawals are immediately finalized” and replayability must be built above the Portal (`OptimismPortal2.sol:639-657`).
2. Pinned v9 tests intentionally assert finalized failure and no delivery for a reverting direct target, including Lockbox mode (`test/L1/OptimismPortal2.t.sol:1648-1719`).
3. The victim used a direct Portal withdrawal with a gas floor below the target's actual requirement. A correct floor makes the outer call revert before an under-gassed CALL; target state can change, but that is the documented direct-call risk.
4. This behavior predates U21; the v9 change deliberately narrows rollback to genuine messenger withdrawals.
