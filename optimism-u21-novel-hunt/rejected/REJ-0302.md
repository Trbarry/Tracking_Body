REJ-0302 — Unlock without finalization through the new `OptimismPortal_L1CDMCallFailed` revert

Idea: v9 reverts when an L2CDM withdrawal's call fails (`OptimismPortal2.sol:651-653`). If the unlock at `:614` survived that revert, ETH would sit in the portal with the withdrawal still replayable.

Killing fact: the revert is a transaction-level revert of the portal frame, so the `unlockETH` call, `finalizedWithdrawals` and `l2Sender` writes are all rolled back. `test_C_L1CDMCallFailed_keepsWithdrawalReplayable_mockedRevert` (messenger forced to revert with `vm.mockCallRevert`): lockbox balance unchanged, portal balance 0, `finalizedWithdrawals == false`, `l2Sender == 0x…dEaD`; the same withdrawal then finalizes once and the lockbox drops by exactly its value.

Also checked: `failedMessages[hash]` cannot become true without the portal finalizing in the same tx (first relay requires `msg.sender == portal`, `L1CrossDomainMessenger.sol:136-138`), so `assert(!failedMessages[hash])` (`CrossDomainMessenger.sol:243`) cannot be turned into a permanent revert by a third party.
