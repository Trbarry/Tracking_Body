# REJ-2504 — stale array or duplicate growth causes portal-wide DoS

## Idea

Deletion leaves `proofSubmitters[hash]` stale, and repeated proving appends duplicates. An attacker could grow it until finalization or deletion becomes unusable.

## Killing fact

The stale/duplicate state is real: prove overwrites the mapping and unconditionally pushes (`OptimismPortal2.sol:480-487`); delete clears only the mapping (`:725`). But source-wide search finds no on-chain array consumer other than the generated index getter and `numProofSubmitters` (`:800-802`). Finalization directly indexes the nested mapping by the explicit address and has constant work independent of array length (`:603-607,671-705`). Duplicate append also predates U21 in v8 (`OptimismPortal2.sol:465-472`).

Impact is paid state growth or naive off-chain integration failure, not protocol-wide withdrawal blocking. The U21 notice tells proof trackers to process `WithdrawalProofDeleted`.

Disposition: Rejected under the engagement's direct-impact standard.
