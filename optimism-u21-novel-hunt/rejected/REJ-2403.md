# REJ-2403 — Delete/re-prove clears replay protection

## Idea

Delete a proven record after blacklisting/challenger victory, re-prove under another game, and finalize again.

## Killing fact

`deleteProvenWithdrawal` deletes only `provenWithdrawals[hash][submitter]` (`OptimismPortal2.sol:711-727`). It never touches the hash-global `finalizedWithdrawals`. Re-proof overwrites/reset-timers only the proof record (`:480-487`). Therefore deletion can restore proof availability before first finalization, but cannot restore execution after finalization. The deployed-code deletion/recreation path is exercised at `mirror/lane-24/tests/WithdrawalFinalization24.t.sol:497-522`.

The append-only `proofSubmitters` list can retain stale/duplicate entries, but no Portal transition iterates it; this is an off-chain tooling nuisance rather than replay or permanent freeze.
