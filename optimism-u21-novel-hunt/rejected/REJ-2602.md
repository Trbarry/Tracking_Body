# REJ-2602 — permissionless CGT transition freezes existing native liquidity

**Hypothesis.** Because CUSTOM_GAS_TOKEN is reversible and read at execution time, an attacker can flip it after deposits/proofs exist and block native-value operations.

**Killed by.** `SystemConfig.setFeature` calls `_assertOnlyProxyAdminOrProxyAdminOwner`; both an arbitrary caller and the SystemConfig owner reverted on the deployed OP proxy. Standard OPCM additionally rejects disabled-to-enabled CGT during an upgrade. The PA owner can force the transition and the fork demonstrates the immediate Portal behavior, but that role can already upgrade the Portal/SystemConfig and privileged misuse is suppressed.

**Exact evidence.** `src/L1/SystemConfig.sol:528-568`; `src/universal/ProxyAdminOwnedBase.sol:102-105`; `src/L1/opcm/OPContractsManagerV2.sol:998-1008`; `FeatureStateMachineTest.test_unprivilegedAndSystemConfigOwnerCannotSetFeatures` and `.test_cgtAndUnknownFeaturesRemainTwoWayToggles`.
