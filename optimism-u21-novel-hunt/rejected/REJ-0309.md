REJ-0309 — Withdrawal with `sender == 0x…dEaD` disables both re-entrancy guards

Idea: if `_tx.sender == DEFAULT_L2_SENDER`, then `l2Sender` stays at the default during the target call (`OptimismPortal2.sol:618`), so nested finalization and `ETHLockbox.unlockETH`'s `l2Sender` check would both pass.

Killing facts:
- The sender is the L2 `msg.sender` of `L2ToL1MessagePasser.initiateWithdrawal`. Reaching `0x…dEaD` needs its private key, or an L1 contract at `0x…dEaD − 0x1111…1111` for the deposit alias (`OptimismPortal2.sol:782-785`); both are 160-bit preimage problems.
- Even then, the lockbox is still excluded as a target (`:599-601`) and each nested finalization needs its own proof and sets its own `finalizedWithdrawals` flag before paying.
