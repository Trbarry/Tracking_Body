REJ-1208 — "ETH pushed onto the portal (donateETH) or an unlock requested while a withdrawal is executing breaks the
lockbox accounting that every chain now depends on"

Killing facts (fork, test/poc/lane12/Lane12.t.sol):
- `lockETH`, `unlockETH`, `receiveLiquidity` from an unauthorized caller -> `ETHLockbox_Unauthorized`; the lockbox has
  no receive/fallback, a plain transfer fails (test_auth_siblings).
- `portal.donateETH{value: 1 ether}` leaves 1 ETH on the portal and the lockbox untouched; a following 2 ETH messenger
  withdrawal unlocks exactly 2 ETH from the lockbox, delivers 2 ETH, and the donated 1 ETH is still on the portal
  (test_auth_siblings). The donation is swept only by the owner-only `migrateLiquidity`
  (`ProxyAdminOwnedBase_NotProxyAdminOwner` for anyone else).
- A withdrawal target that re-enters `finalizeWithdrawalTransactionExternalProof` for a second proven withdrawal is
  stopped by the portal guard (`OptimismPortal_NoReentrancy`, v9:OptimismPortal2.sol:594) before `unlockETH` is
  reached (test_unlockETH_duringWithdrawal_blocked); the lockbox has its own `l2Sender` guard behind it
  (v9:src/L1/ETHLockbox.sol:169-171).
- Failed non-messenger withdrawal: value unlocked and re-locked in the same call, lockbox balance unchanged
  (test_R05_sibling_otherSender_failure_isFinalizedAsFailed).
Same conclusion as lane 09 REJ-0902.
