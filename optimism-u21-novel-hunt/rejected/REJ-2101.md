Hypothesis: One chain received a sibling from another chain, or retained an old portal/lockbox/ASR/DGF/WETH reference.

Killing fact: At block N, all 283 zero-input ABI getters returning an address across the 41 proxies matched an independently constructed same-chain/shared topology. The same values remained at block 26433900. Each lockbox authorized exactly its own portal (diagonal four-by-four matrix), no lockbox authorized any of the other current lockboxes, and every nonzero ASR anchor game was registered in that chain's DGF. See `mirror/lane-21/artifacts/post-upgrade-wiring.json` (zero expectation mismatches and zero configuration changes).

Source support: OPCMv2 constructs each chain-local initializer from its loaded `ChainContracts` at `OPContractsManagerV2.sol:831-959`. ETHLockbox only adds the supplied portals at `ETHLockbox.sol:94-108`.

Disposition: Rejected. Later ASR anchor-game movement is ordinary registered game progression, not pointer drift.
