REJ-1002 — "U21 freezes or lets someone steal bonds of in-flight dispute games" (DelayedWETH / ASR swapped under clones)

Idea: games created before block 26430859 hold 0.08 ETH bonds in DelayedWETH proxy `0x1b6dbf05…`, upgraded
1.5.1 -> 2.0.0 with a new pause pointer; ASR upgraded 3.9.0 -> 4.0.0 and `systemConfig()` removed. An unset
lockbox, a removed getter or a changed pause identifier could block `closeGame`/`claimCredit`, or the new
`hold`/`recover`/`unlock` wiring could let a third party take credit.

Killing facts:
- `weth.ethLockbox()` = `asr.ethLockbox()` = `portal.ethLockbox()` on OP Mainnet (`0x322b47Ff…`), Ink, Unichain;
  Soneium has only type 5 (no bonds). Lockbox address and `ETH_LOCKBOX` were already in place at N-1, so
  `paused()` resolves to the same SuperchainConfig identifier as before.
- SuperFaultDisputeGame 0.8.0 only calls `getAnchorRoot`, `respectedGameType`, `paused`, `isGameFinalized`,
  `setAnchorState`, `isGameProper` on the ASR and `deposit`, `unlock`, `withdraw(address,uint256)` on the WETH
  (`SuperFaultDisputeGame.sol:282,333,340,583,966,978,993,1014,1022,1025`); none of them changed selector or meaning.
- `DelayedWETH.withdraw` still debits `withdrawals[msg.sender][_guy]` and pays `msg.sender` (the game);
  `hold`/`recover` are `proxyAdminOwner()`-only (`DelayedWETH.sol:114,130`).
- Fork test `test/poc/lane10/InflightGames.t.sol::test_inflight_games_payout_postU21` at block 26433900:
  67 pre-U21 games with a WETH balance (25 in progress, 29 resolved-not-closed, 13 closed-unclaimed) are driven
  through `resolveClaim` / `resolve` / `closeGame` / `claimCredit` x2 with `vm.warp`; all 67 pay the full balance
  to the claimant and leave 0 WETH in the game. Control on `l1pre`: 60/60.

Not covered: games with more than one claim (none in the sample), and DelayedWETH proxies of retired game
types on other chains. Bonds are in any case carved out of Critical/High.
