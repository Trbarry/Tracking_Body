REJ-0202 — A new v9 field is left zero on one of the four chains (ETHLockbox.superchainConfig, ASR.ethLockbox, DelayedWETH.ethLockbox, Portal.ethLockbox)

Idea: init versions did not change between v8 and v9 (ETHLockbox/ASR/DelayedWETH = 1), so if the upgrade had used a plain
`upgradeTo`, or skipped a chain's contract, `ETHLockbox.paused()` would call address(0) and revert, bricking deposits'
unlock path, proofs, finalisation and bond withdrawals; or a chain that kept ETH in the portal would end up with
`ethLockbox == 0` while v9 requires it (`_assertValidLockboxState`, OptimismPortal2.sol:839-843).
