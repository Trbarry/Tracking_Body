the message to memory before the gas check. A griefer finalizes with the portal's minimum gas so that the
messenger records every withdrawal as failed (or runs out of gas -> L1CDMCallFailed).

when the portal's `hasMinGas` passes the messenger still has > minGas*64/63 + 190,000.

L1CDMCallFailed (2), no unrecorded (6) window exists today. (GasSweep.t.sol, Fab2.t.sol::test_big_*.)
