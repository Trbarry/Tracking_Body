# REJ-3104 — Reprove/delete/external-proof paths bypass withdrawal uniqueness

**Idea.** Multiple proof submitters, same-submitter reprove, `finalizeWithdrawalTransactionExternalProof`, or permissionless deletion could enable a second delivery or erase a valid victim proof.

**Killing fact.** Real proof replay by another submitter created isolated mapping state and did not alter the original timestamp. Same-submitter reprove reset only its own timer and appended a duplicate list entry. After either proof finalized, both direct and external-proof replay reverted because `finalizedWithdrawals[hash]` is global (`OptimismPortal2.sol:603-610,671-679`). Valid live proof deletion reverted; only the challenger-win proof deleted (`:711-727`). Reproduced at exact N-1 and N by `test_exactNMinus1ToN_reproveBehaviorSame` and on later v9 by `test_reproveIsolationAndFinalizationUniqueness_realProof`.

The duplicate submitter list is PRIM-3103, but no current onchain loop or economic impact was found.
