REJ-0304 — Target burns all gas so the relock cannot run: finalized, unlocked, ETH stuck in portal

Idea: for a direct withdrawal, make the target consume the forwarded 63/64 so the portal lacks gas for `lockETH` (`OptimismPortal2.sol:661-665`) after `finalizedWithdrawals` and `unlockETH` already happened.

Killing fact: running out of gas after the call reverts the whole transaction, including the unlock and the finalized flag. `test_D_direct_targetBurnsAllGas_relockStillFits` sweeps tx gas for the real 53.47 ETH fee-vault withdrawal (gasLimit 400,000) with a gas-burning target (etched):

| tx gas | outcome | lockbox | portal | finalized |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 480k, 520k | revert | unchanged | 0 | false |
| 560k, 600k, 800k, 3M | finalized, success=false | unchanged (exact relock) | 0 | true |

No tested gas value produced "finalized and not relocked".

Related: forcing the call to fail by call depth is out of reach; with the 63/64 rule a frame holding the required ~140k gas can be at most about 385 deep from a 60M-gas transaction.
